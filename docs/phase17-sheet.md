# Phase 17: the Hollow, the Auger, the grip, and the Source (the sheet)

*The plan was written before building; "As built" at the end says what came of it.*

The bottom of the mountain. Four things: the last part of the Deep, its keeper, what the keeper leaves, and what is
past it.

## The grip, and rough rock

The Auger's gift. With it he clings to rough rock, and climbs it.

- **Rough rock** is a material of its own: the same pale, chisel-bitten face wherever it is, so it can be known on
  sight. It is rock in every other way.
- **To climb:** go into a rough face and keep going. He takes hold and goes up it. **Down** takes him down it. **Jump**
  throws him off it, away from the wall. Pressing away lets go. At the top of a face he pulls himself up onto it.
- He can take hold from the ground or out of the air (a jump, a fall, a glide). Hurt, he lets go.
- **Never ice, brass or glass,** as the plan says: only rough rock.
- **Where it is, in this build:** in the Hollow (the walls of the Auger's pit, and the wall beyond the Source). The
  plan wants rough faces in every region from the Roots on, seen long before they can be climbed. Those are not in
  this build: they change rooms that are finished, and want choosing one by one. They belong with phase 18 (the Shard
  is reached by the grip) and are listed there.

## The Hollow

*The Deep, under the floor of the Great Shaft. Fourteen screens. Dark. Reached only by the Shaft's floor, so only
with the Wyrm's wind.*

Under the mountain there is a hollow nobody made, older than the house, older than the flame. The Auger broke into
its roof. The guild came down after it and were not heard from. The engine is still turning.

**The way in.** The hole in the floor of the Shaft, where the warm light comes up. He drops through it.

**Rooms.**

1. **The roof** (two screens, one over the other). Where the Shaft comes out, under water falling from above in long
   threads. Broken ledges of the roof, down. At its foot the falling water gathers: the level's raising water.
2. **The guild's last camp** (two screens). Tents, a fire long out, a table, and on it the last page of the tally:
   a depth, and under it, in the same hand as every other entry for two hundred years, one word. Guild lamps, unlit;
   he lights them. **Echoes**: the Vein's, and more of them. Nobody speaks here. Hob is not here; he said he would be
   further on, and this is further than he went.
3. **The circles** (three screens: the vast one). The floor of the Hollow, torn in rings by the engine going round.
   Ruts to cross, ridges to climb, and the dark on every side. Echoes. Far off in it, low and warm, a light.
4. **The bore** (four screens, two by two). The keeper's hall: a pit with three floors, one under another.
5. **The Source** (two screens; open once the Auger is still). Below.
6. **The wall** (one screen). Beyond the Source: a rough face going up out of sight. With the grip he climbs it, and
   comes out at the Pithead, in the Roots. The way home.

**Light.** Dark, his lamp, the guild's lamps he lights; and then the one warm light, which the Deep has not prepared
him for.

**Underneath.** The bottom. The fear of what is under everything; finding that there is a floor, and that someone was
there first. The light was never only at the top.

**The relic** (in the camp): **the Tally's End**, the guild's last leaf. *Once each rest, an Echo that catches him
does not hurt him.*

## The Auger

*The Deep's keeper. Drawn in code, in pieces: a hull, a boiler's glow, four iron legs, and the bit.*

The guild's engine: a great drill on iron legs, still boring for a vein that ran out two hundred years ago. Its order
was *deeper*.

**Its hall.** The bore is a pit. He comes in at its top corner and drops to the first floor. There are three floors,
seven of his heights apart. Its walls are rough rock, which means nothing to him yet.

**How it fights.** It walks after him on its legs, slowly, its bit turning.

- **The tear.** It marks where he stands (the floor cracks under him, red), raises the bit, and drives it down. The
  floor there bursts: a blow, and spoil thrown up either side.
- **The bite.** After two or three tears it drives the bit in deep, and it sticks. Its hull comes down low. **This
  is the opening:** he gets up onto its back and strikes the collar where the bit joins it. A little while, then it
  wrenches free and throws him off.
- **Spoil** (from two thirds of its strength): it flings rock from the bit in arcs; each falls on a mark.
- Its legs and its turning bit hurt to touch. Its collar is shut (blows ring off it) except while it is bitten in.

**The floor goes.** At two thirds of its strength, and again at one third, it stops, rears, and bores straight down:
the whole floor cracks, then goes, and he and it fall together to the floor below. It lands hard and is slow to
rise: free blows. The fight goes on, quicker, one floor down. The third floor is the Hollow's own, and holds.

**Its fall.** The bit breaks. It stops, for the first time in two hundred years. *The Auger is still. Its order was:
deeper. There is no deeper.* What gathers out of it and comes to him: **the grip.** The first rough rock he can
climb is the wall of the pit he is standing in.

**Its entrance,** to match the others:

| Beat | The Auger's |
|---|---|
| Seal | The guild's shoring comes down across the way in: stone slabs, as the Warden's (it is the guild's stone) |
| Dread | A grinding under the floor, going round him, nearer each turn; the floor shudders in a ring; his lamp gutters; grit comes down. Then it stops |
| Rise | The bit comes up through the far floor, spinning, throwing spoil. The hull hauls itself out after it, a leg at a time. It rears to its full height. It drives the bit down into the floor |
| Reveal | On that blow: its name. Over it: KEEPER OF THE DEEP. Under it: ITS ORDER WAS: DEEPER |
| Burst | The floor quakes, a ring, spoil from one side of the hall to the other |

## The Source

Past the pit, a low passage, and then a wide quiet room.

- **A spring rising in the floor,** and **beside it a flame burning low**, kept by no one he can see. It is the same
  light as the flames above. It is the only warm thing in the Deep.
- **At it:** he is mended, and his flask is filled **to the brim: it holds one draught more, for good.** It is a
  water: he can rest there and travel from there.
- **A page**, once, when he first stands in it. No one speaks. It says what he sees: the spring, the flame, and that
  the water goes up from here.
- **What it means for the ending** is the next phase's (the Source found before the Fallen or after reads
  differently). This build only records that he has been here.

## What is checked

- Rough rock: without the grip it is rock; with it he climbs it, comes down it, leaps from it, and pulls up onto its
  top. Both ways the checks work out where he can get to learn it.
- The Hollow: every room reached with what he has when he comes; the Source and the wall shut until the Auger is
  still; nowhere to be stuck, the pit with its floors gone included (the way out of it is the grip).
- The Auger: its tear warns and then hurts; its collar turns blows except while bitten in; each floor goes at its
  mark and both are set down unhurt; it is still for good once beaten; if he falls, the floors are back.
- The Source: the draught, once. The wall's passage comes out in the Roots.
- The usual: a relic, two threads, its page in the Annals, voices, every marker in open air, every runback.

## For the player to try (the playtest list)

1. The Auger: is it clear when it can be hurt? Is getting onto its back a good moment or a fiddly one?
2. The floor going: is it a thrill or a muddle? Can you tell it is coming?
3. After it falls: do you find, without being told, that the pit's wall can be climbed now?
4. Climbing: is going into a wall to go up it natural? Is letting go by accident a problem?
5. The Source: after everything above, does it land? Is one page enough, or too much?
6. The wall back to the Roots: a good way home, or just a long climb?

## As built

*Build 74.* Built as above, with these differences and details.

- **The grip.** As planned. He climbs at a little over half his run, comes down faster than he goes up, and the leap
  off a wall is a full jump away from it. Let go of the key and he hangs where he is; it takes pressing away, or a
  jump, to leave the wall. Rough rock is a pale, chisel-bitten strip one tile wide down the face of the rock.
- **The Hollow** is fourteen screens: the roof (two), the camp (two), the circles (three), the bore (four), the
  Source (two), the wall (one). Thin water falls in three threads through the roof room and gathers at its foot.
  Five Echoes: two in the camp, three in the circles. Seven guild lamps to light.
- **The tally's last page** is on the camp's table. Standing by it says what is on it: a depth, and one word. The
  word itself is on the relic taken from beside it (the Tally's End): ENOUGH.
- **The Auger** is as planned. Thirty strength. Three tears and then a bite on its first floor; two and a bite on the
  lower two. While it is bitten in he has about three and a half seconds, and it will take nine before it wrenches
  free whatever is left. Its floors go at twenty and at ten. Spoil is thrown from the second floor on, once after
  the first tear of each round. Its bit hurts to touch only while it bores a floor away; a foot hurts only where it
  comes down. **Its back is a real thing to stand on** and it carries him as it moves.
- **Its entrance** is the others' shape: the guild's slabs across the way in; the grinding going round him under the
  floor, nearer each turn; then it comes up through the floor at the far side of the hall, hull after bit, rears,
  and drives the bit down, and its name lands on that blow.
- **After it:** the grip is given as the keepers' gifts are. The pit's east wall is rough from its foot to the way
  in, and goes on up into the circles, so the first climb is out of its hall. The way on to the Source opens at the
  pit's foot.
- **The Source:** a spring in the floor, the low flame beside it (the brightest and only warm light in the level),
  one page, his flask one draught deeper for good, and a water to rest and travel at.
- **The wall** beyond it: twenty rows of rough face in a chimney, a stance part way, and at its top he comes out at
  the Pithead in the Roots. That way is one way.
- **Art:** none bought. The Auger, its floors and the rough face are drawn in code.

**What is weaker than the sheet.**

- **He has no drawing for climbing.** On a wall he is shown in his falling pose, turned to the wall. It reads, but it
  is not a climb. A proper one is on the art list.
- **Rough rock is only in the Hollow.** The plan's faces in every region from the Roots on are not built (see the
  top of this sheet). Until they are, the grip opens the pit's wall, the wall home, and nothing else.
- **The Auger's wreck is not left in the pit.** Come back later and the pit is empty.
- **It cannot be watched from the title's list of keepers** (that list is the six of the climb).
- **Its hall is very dark.** It is lit by his lamp, its boiler, its open collar and the red of its marks. That may be
  right and may be too little; it wants looking at on a real screen.
- **The Hollow has one kind of creature,** as its plan says (Echoes), and nobody to speak to. The checks that ask
  three kinds and a person of every region are told so for this one.
- Nothing here has been played by a person. A keeper most of all needs that.
