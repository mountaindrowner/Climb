# Phase 12: the Tempest's wings (the sheet)

Two wings (`docs/roadmap.md`): **the Wreck of the Works** (west, open to anyone) and **the Eye of the Storm** (east and
above, behind the Wyrm's wind). This sheet is the Wreck, built in build 66. The Eye is the next sheet.

## The Wreck of the Works

*Tempest, west · 9 screens · open*

**What it was.** The Wrights built a works on the storm's edge to catch the lightning in jars and coils and keep it,
and so make their own flame. The storm took it apart.

**The question at its door, and the answer before he leaves:** *can it be turned off?* No. A lever only sends it
somewhere else. It stops when the thing they kept it in is broken.

### Its rule: the current is always somewhere

The Galleries were about what he chooses to light. This is about where he chooses to put what he cannot stop.

- **The works' iron is in two halves:** copper, and steel. The current runs in one half at a time.
- **To stand on the live half is to be shocked.** It costs a heart; it does not bar the way.
- **A lever, struck, sends the current to the other half:** every deck and every coil in the works at once. The iron
  behind him changes too.
- **Nothing turns it off.** When the Jar is broken all of it is dead, for good: no iron shocks, the coils do not arc,
  the arms hang still, and the grates lift.

Nothing says this. The yard shows it: one lever on the ground, and copper rungs up to a chest.

### How it is entered, and left

- **Out of the landing's west wall,** at the foot of the Tempest. No gift is needed.
- **The way out is the cable run:** a grate in the jar-house's east wall, into the watch, by Ordric's flame. It lifts
  when the Jar is broken.

### What is in it

None of it is a creature, and all of it is drawn in code.

| What | What it does | How it is read, and answered |
|---|---|---|
| **Lever** (9) | Struck: the current changes half | Its handle leans the way the current is |
| **Coil** (3) | While the current is in its half it throws an arc to its other post, every 2.8 s | 0.7 s of thin flicker first. Be past it, or over it; or send the current away |
| **Wright's-arm** (3) | A crane's hook on its chain, swinging across a deck; it strikes what is in its way | It is seen coming. It cannot be struck |
| **Sparks** (3) | As in the Tempest | As in the Tempest |
| **The Jar** (the keeper) | **With the current in the other half it is dull, and the blade rings off it.** With the current in its own half it can be broken, and it fills, in six seconds. Full, **it lets it all go at once: through every piece of iron in the house** | To hurt it he must make it live, and that makes its side of the floor live too: strike it from the steel. As it fills it brightens; 0.9 s of white before it lets go. **Be on stone, or in the air.** Or send the current away, and it drains. Twenty-six cuts |

The Jar is dull when he comes in. The fight starts when he throws a lever.

### The relic: the Wright's Boots

*Live iron does not shock him.* Cork and gum. The Wrights wore them to walk their own works.

### What is said

- The first time a lever is thrown: "the lever sends the current to the other half of the iron".
- When it breaks: **"The Jar is broken: what the Wrights caught has gone back to the storm."**
- The Annals: the Jar, "The Wrights caught the lightning to keep it. It kept them."

## As built (build 66), and what was checked

- **274 automated checks pass**, eight of them the wing's own: its screens; the two halves, the discharge and the
  dead iron; a shock on the live half, and none with the boots; all of it reached with the levers thrown, and the
  drafting room only after the Jar; the lever; the coil; the arm; the Jar dull, live, filling, letting go, draining.
- **Played through by the test browser:** a copper rung shocking him, the lever thrown, the same rung safe; a coil's
  arc landing; an arm landing; the Jar ignoring a blow while dull, then live and taking blows, filling, and its
  discharge reaching him on the steel; its breaking, the iron dead afterwards, out by the cable door into the watch,
  and into the drafting room.
- **Found and fixed while building:** the yard's cliff closed the coils' door; there was open sky over the wing that he
  could be stranded in; the Jar came back whole on a second visit.
- **Not as I first wrote it:** I had a check saying the works cannot be got through without a lever. That is false:
  live iron costs hearts and does not bar the way, so someone willing to be shocked can cross it. The check was
  wrong, not the game; it is corrected.
- **Not checked:** nothing here was played by hand. The sounds. The climb past the cranes' door to the strand. The
  boots in play. Whether six seconds is the right time for the Jar to fill.
- **Nine screens, not twelve,** and no "cable run" room: the way out is a door into the watch.
- **A save is moved:** the Tempest is two rooms further east than it was.
