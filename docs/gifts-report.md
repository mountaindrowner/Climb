# What each gift opens

Generated by `node tools/gifts-report.mjs` from the real motor (`tests/helpers/reach.mjs`). Each region is played
with the gifts he has when he first comes to it, then with each later gift added in the order he is given them.
"Places" are spots he can stand on. A gift that opens nothing he has walked past shows here as nothing: that is
what docs/metroidvania.md sets out to change. Runbacks are the shortest way from a flame to where the region leads, at a run; over 40 s is marked long.
