# Phase 18: the throw, the Deep climbed, the Shard, and the end (the sheet)

*Written 27 Nov, on your word to go on with the phases. Nothing here is built. It puts together the roadmap's phase 18
(the Shard, the end of the story), what phase 17 carried over (rough faces in the regions already built), and your
decision of 26 Nov that he is thrown into the Deep (roadmap, 0.26: "Its sheet comes before it is built"). The lines
anyone speaks are not in it: they come with each build, for your yes.*

## What this does to the shape of the game

- **Today:** six regions, one above another. The Deep is shut. The game says it is optional.
- **After:** five regions up. At the door of the sixth he is thrown down. He climbs out of the Deep from its bottom.
  Then the sixth region, and the end. The Shard is for whoever has the grip.
- **The Deep stops being optional.** The way out of it is about forty-five screens: the Hollow, the Great Shaft, the
  Vein, the Workings. The Flooded Levels stay a side way off it.

## 1. The throw

Your words: "As soon as the second-to-last keeper falls, and before he enters the last region, the last keeper taunts
him and throws him down to the very lowest part of the Deep. He climbs back up through it. Until then it stays shut."

- **Where.** In the Firmament's first room. Mother Thorn is down; he has come up the passage. The
  Fallen is standing in the way.
- **What is seen.** It has his walk and his shape, dark, a pale thread at its neck. It says one thing. Then it takes
  him and throws him off the mountain.
- **The fall.** The way the game began: the whole height of the mountain going by. This time the water at the
  bottom does not give him back. He goes through it, down the Well (the Roots' Sounding: "It has no floor that we
  have found"), and comes out of the roof of the Hollow in the falling water.
- **Where he lands.** At the Hollow's own water, at the foot of its roof. Whole, with everything he has.
- **Until he has climbed out once:**
  - there is no way back up by the water;
  - a death gives him back at the last water he rested at in the Deep, as anywhere.
- **The Deep's doors in the Roots stay shut until then.** After, they are open both ways, and the Deep is a place
  like any other.

## 2. The Deep, climbed from the bottom

It was built to be gone down. Two ways out, and I would leave both open:

- **On, through the Auger.** The camp, the rings, the Auger, the Source, and the rough wall home with the grip. The
  short way and the hard one. It is the only way to the grip, and so to the Shard.
- **Back up, the way the guild came down.** The roof's ledges, the Shaft's floor under its water, the camp's stages
  up the drop in the cold air, the Vein from its far end, the seal, the Workings, the cage (which runs now), the
  Pithead. The long way. The Assayer's hall is on it, come into from behind.

What has to be made for that:

- **Three of the four joins go upward already.** Checked 27 Nov with the tests' player, without the grip: from where
  he lands in the Hollow to its way out above; from the Shaft's floor to its head; from the Workings' seal to the
  Pithead.
- **The fourth is the Vein.** With the Assayer down it goes upward as it is. With the Assayer standing it is not
  known: its hall is shut behind grates until it falls, and nothing has ever come at them from the far side. So
  either the Assayer has to fall on the long way, or its grates are made to open from within (question 2).
- **The words.** "This place is optional. To go on with the climb, go back up" goes. The goal in the pause menu says
  where he is and that the way is up.
- **Hob.** He is met going down, each time further down, and his lines say so. Coming up, he is met in the other
  order. His lines want writing again for that, by decision 0.27 (never a giveaway, never a poem).
- **The Deep's far ends** (build 120) need nothing: they are off the way.

## 3. Rough faces in the regions already built

Carried from phase 17: "rough faces in every region from the Roots on, seen long before they can be climbed."

- **One or two in each of the six regions,** cut into walls that are there. About ten in all.
- **Each is seen from the main way** and leads somewhere small: a thread, a chest, a short way round.
- **One of them is the way to the Shard:** the west face under the summit.
- **Which walls** is a list for its own build, room by room, with pictures before it goes in.

## 4. The Shard

From `docs/world.md`: "It broke from the top the night he fell and has not come down. Unexplained, and it stays so...
a piece of the mountain hanging in the air with a little of the old light still in it. No one is here. Left alone on
purpose."

- **Six screens, three places,** west of the summit and above it:
  - **The underside.** Rough faces under the summit's west lip, with air between them. He climbs one, throws himself
    off it, and takes hold of the next.
  - **The gap.** Nothing under him. The longest glide in the game, with one thing in it for each gift he has: a pane
    to go through with the dash, a crust to break with the hammer, a face to catch at the far end.
  - **The shard.** A piece of the mountain, the right way up, with a room in it lit warm. The only warm light above
    the Gardens.
- **No fight. No one. Nothing written.** Nobody was ever here to write it.
- **It can be gone to before the Fallen or after,** by whoever has the grip.
- **What is in it** is yours to say (question 4).

## 5. The end

- **The Fallen's fight stays as it is.**
- **The ending, read two ways,** and never needing the second:
  - **He has stood at the Source:** the light comes down the scar to the bottom, and the picture shows the small
    flame it reaches, which he has seen.
  - **He has not:** the light goes down into the water and the picture does not show where it ends.
  - The five lines stay. Any change to them is yours.
- **The three, once more each, at the summit, after the Fallen and before the beacon:**
  - **Mirel,** sitting by the empty cradle, without her shell. Unhurried. She has stopped counting.
  - **Corwen,** his book open on the stone at its last page, and nothing on it. (If he has stood at the Source: the
    depth of it, and nothing else.)
  - **Ordric,** with a lamp he has not lit. It was never his to light.
- **After the end:** the title shows the light down the scar. And he wakes at the summit's water with the mountain
  his to walk (question 5).
- **A second climb with something changed:** not in this phase. It stays an idea (`docs/circle.md`).

## What I am not sure of

- **The Deep is long for a place he did not choose to go.** Forty-five screens, most of them dark. That is what the
  throw asks; but it is the biggest change to the game since the wings.
- **The long way up skips the Auger.** Then he reaches the end without the grip, and the Shard is still ahead of
  him. I think that is right (it is a reason to go back down). You may want the Auger to be the only way out.
- **The Fallen is seen an hour or more before it is fought.** That is the point of the throw. It also spends the
  surprise of its shape early.
- **The Assayer from behind** may be a worse fight than from the front, or no fight at all. If it has to fall on the
  long way, then both ways out have a keeper on them: the Auger on one, the Assayer on the other.

## To decide

1. **The Deep on the main way, about forty-five screens: yes?** It is what 0.26 says. I am asking because of its
   size.
2. **Two ways out, or only through the Auger?** I would leave both. And on the long way: **must the Assayer fall,**
   or may he slip out past it? I would have it fall: a climb out of the Deep with nothing in the way is a walk.
3. **Where the throw happens:** in the Firmament's first room (as above), or at the head of the Gardens, in sight of
   Mother Thorn's fallen hall?
4. **What is in the Shard?**
   - (a) Only the light and the view.
   - (b) The light, and a last heart.
   - (c) Something that changes the end.
   I would do (a), with one thread on the way across.
5. **May he walk the mountain after the end,** or does the game return to the title, as now?
6. **The second climb:** leave it out of this phase?
