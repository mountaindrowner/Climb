# Phase 8: the look of the world, and the people (the sheet)

For approval before building (`docs/roadmap.md`, the finish line). Two builds: **8a** the people and the look; **8b**
their voices, and Corwen's map.

## What is there now

- **All three people stand in every region:** 18 meetings on the climb, and Hob in the Workings. Nothing says which
  meetings the story needs, which the player needs, and which are extras; some stand off the path where they can be
  missed.
- **Platforms** are the same plank in every region.
- **Torches** are moved off the people (phase 1), but nothing checks that each is mounted on something.
- **Ordric's shop** is a list of buttons.
- **The map** shows rooms seen and marks; it does not show ways he could not take.

## 8a. The people

**The rule (your direction): they are there when he needs them.** Nobody the story or the player needs is rare, hidden
or behind something. Hidden places are extras on top, never the only way to meet someone. So every meeting is one of
three kinds, and each kind has its own rule:

| Kind | What it is for | The rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Story** | A beat the story needs: who they are, what has changed for them | **On the main path, where he must pass.** It cannot be missed. If he walks by without talking, the next meeting carries the missed beat in a line, so the story never has a hole |
| **Service** | Something the player needs: Ordric's shop; Corwen's count of what is left to find | **Always close.** Ordric is beside every region's raising water, where he is raised and rests. Corwen's count is on the map itself once he has been met |
| **Their own place** | An extra: more of who they are, and a small reward | **Off the path, to be looked for.** Missing it costs nothing the story or the climb needs |

### Where each is found, and why (build 54)

Nobody stands in the open middle of a room. Each has a place **cut into rock at an edge**: under a shelf of rock,
against a wall, in a hollow. Each of the three has one kind of place, and the kind says who they are.

**Ordric: a lamp-room at every raising water.** He keeps the lamps by the water because he was asked to, and no one
has told him to stop. So his place was made for a keeper before he came: a room cut into the rock beside each water,
with shelves for lamps. It says the water was always meant to be tended, and that someone prepared a place for whoever
would do it. It faces the water, because everyone the water gives back comes up in front of his door. The way on goes
over his roof: he is passed every time, never sought.

| Region | His lamp-room | Why there |
|---|---|---|
| The Roots | In the rock past the corridor's spike pit, before the climb | Where the well-keepers trimmed their lamps before going up. The first room is left to the waking alone |
| The Cloister | At the water's edge under the crypt stair | The founders cut one by every water |
| The Spires | In the gate's own rock, beyond the meltwater | The gate was the last kept place on the road |
| The Tempest | Dug into the scree under the hut | Out of the rain, the only way a flame lasts there |
| The Gardens | In the fallen wall's cellar, under its stones | The garden wall had a lamp-store; the wall fell, the store did not |
| The Firmament | Under the foot of the long stair | His last lamp, at the bottom of the last stair: he has never gone up it |

**Mirel: a scrape, always shallow, always on the way up.** She climbs to arrive deserving it and will not rest
properly, so she never has a room: only a hollow just deep enough to sit in, facing the way on. As her story turns, her
shelter goes: by the Gardens she has put the shell down and sits in the open, and at the top there is nothing over her
at all.

| Region | Her place | Why there |
|---|---|---|
| The Roots | The roofed gate at the near end of the bridge | Over the chasm he fell down: "You fell past me" |
| The Cloister | Under the first turn of the bell stair, at the wall furthest from the door | A penitent's place: the corner of the tower, counting |
| The Spires | The hollow under the leaking rock | "A crack in the rock with a draught through it." The leak is on top of the rock, on the way across |
| The Tempest | A lip of the cliff under a brow of rock, at the storm's door | The last dry place. She cannot carry the shell further |
| The Gardens | In the open, among the flowers | She has put it down. She needs no roof |
| The Firmament | In the open, where she can see the top | "This is as far as I go" |

**Corwen: a cell, raised, looking down.** He records the mountain exactly and takes no part in it. So he is always
set apart and above: a reading cell cut into a wall over a doorway, where he can see who comes and write it down.

| Region | His cell | Why there |
|---|---|---|
| The Roots | A shelf of rock over the chamber's east door, off the second plank | He watches who comes up out of the water |
| The Cloister | A stone gallery over the nave's west door | Where the house's hours were written down |
| The Spires, the Tempest, the Gardens | His nooks, behind a crawl (the extras) | Where he keeps what he has written, out of the weather |
| The Firmament | A cell in the orrery's east wall, where its arms pass | He has charted every star from here but one |

## 8a. The look

- **Platforms in each region's own make:** the Roots timber and root; the Cloister cut stone on corbels; the Spires
  ice-crusted rock; the Tempest riveted iron grating; the Gardens wood bound with vine; the Firmament brass. Drawn in
  code; the same shape and size to stand on.
- **Every torch mounted:** on a wall in a bracket, or on a stand on the floor. A torch with neither is moved to the
  nearest wall or given a stand. A test fails the build if any torch hangs in the air.

## 8b. Voices, and Corwen's map

### Voices

- **Each a clearer character:** Ordric low, slow and warm, with long vowels; Mirel quick, clipped and breathless, her
  lines rising at the end; Corwen dry and even, precise, with small pauses at commas; Hob a gravelly mutter.
- **A hint of the words:** each syllable's start is shaped by its consonant: a hiss for s and f, a pop for p, b, t and
  d, a hum for m and n, a soft roll for r and l. Not speech; the shape of speech.

### Corwen's map

Once Corwen has been met, the map becomes his:
- **Ways he could not take** are marked where he met them, with what stopped them: cracked stone (the spade), a gap
  too wide (the boots), a height (the scale).
- **A mark glows** when a new gift opens it, until he has been through.
- **His falls** are tallied on the region's page.
- **A page is sealed** (a mark on it) when everything on it is found: every room, strand, chest and relic.

Before Corwen is met, the map is as it is now.

### As built (build 55)

- **Voices:** as above (`docs/voices.md` has each voice and the four consonant shapes).
- **Met** means any one talk with Corwen, anywhere: his cell in the Roots, the Cloister's gallery, a nook.
- **Ways he could not take:** only **cracked stone** is marked, because it is the only kind of shut way the mountain
  has so far. He has to have come within a few tiles of it without the spade. Grey and marked SHUT until the Warden
  leaves the spade; then gold, beating, marked OPEN NOW, until he breaks through. Gaps for the boots and heights for
  the scale are not marked: nothing in the six regions is yet built to need them. They come with the one continuous
  mountain (phase 9), where gifts gate the ways between regions.
- **His falls** are counted per region from this build on (falls before it are in the total only).
- **A sealed page:** when every room has been seen and no strand, chest or relic is left, a banner says so once, and
  the page carries a seal from then on.
- Also fixed: the map's first-time hint ran under the BACK button; it now sits beside the map.
