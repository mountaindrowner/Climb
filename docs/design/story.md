# Story and world (open)

> **Answered in depth by [`lore.md`](lore.md).** The notes below were the starting questions.

What we know: a warrior wakes in a pool at the bottom of a mine so deep no one remembers it. The pool will not let him die. Above him, seven Wardens guard the way up. Below the pool, things even the Wardens avoid.

Open questions, in the order they probably need answering:

1. Who is the warrior, and why is he at the bottom? (Fell? Thrown? Went looking?)
2. What is the Pool? The proposed hook: it is why the deep things live down there. They are drawn to it, guard it, or are kept alive by it.
3. What waits at the Peak, and what does reaching it mean?
4. Is the thing hunting you up the shaft one of the Wardens, or older?
5. The final title, and a name for zone 6.

Rules: every name and creature is ours. No Moria, Balrog, Gandalf, Durin, Endless Stair.
