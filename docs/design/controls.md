# Controls

One thumb each side. ▲ over ▼ on the left, B and A on the right set like a controller, the flask between. Keyboard for desktop: W/↑/Space, S/↓, J or Z, K or X, F or Q. Code: `src/core/input.js`, routing in `src/entities/player/player.js`.

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| ▲ | Jump; repair a lift on its landing; grab a root while falling |
| ▼ | Duck under beams and sweeps |
| A | Attack; only damages a Warden while it kneels |
| B | Block; a tap in the last 0.2 s before a hit lands is a parry |
| Flask | Heal 2 |

**Open: the dodge.** Jump and duck are the dodges today. Options on the table: Down plus B to roll; double-tap a direction to dash; a B hybrid where a well-timed press parries and a late press rolls (keeps four buttons).

**Open: steering.** The warrior auto-climbs. If the stair branches, the player needs a way to choose left or right and jump between platforms. Candidates: ▲ while a branch sign is lit takes the side route; a swipe; or the dodge input doubling as a lane change.

**Proposed.** Cooldown ability slots stacked above A and B.
