# The Fungal Caverns (zone 4)

**Feel.** Bioluminescent green, soft and wrong. Ambushes.

**Spawn.** Creatures burst from spore pods. A pod swells before it pops.

**Hazards to design.** Spore clouds that invert a tell; bouncy caps as platforms if the stair branches; a pursuit section with something fast in the dark.

**Warden.** The Spore Warden. Ideas: splits into smaller things at phase 2; the lights go out and only its glow shows.

**Status.** Palette only. Module: `src/zones/fungal.js`.
