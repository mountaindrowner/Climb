# The Deep (zone 1)

**Feel.** Darkness, purple crystal glints, nameless things older than the mine. The tutorial zone, and the one that hints at what lives below the Pool.

**Spawn.** Crawlers come out of glowing cracks in the wall; spitters cling above the stair. Built.

**Hazards.** Broken steps and fallen beams. Built.

**Warden.** Warden of the Deep: slam, sweep, spit, with the kneel as the opening. Built; its moveset is the template every other zone borrows for now.

**Open.** The Pool's lore hook: are the deep things drawn to it, guarding it, or kept alive by it?

Module: `src/zones/deep.js`.
