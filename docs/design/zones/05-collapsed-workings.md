# The Collapsed Workings (zone 5)

**Feel.** Dust, timbers, nothing is stable. The stair itself is the enemy.

**Spawn.** Things drop from above with the cave-ins. The grit falling from the ceiling thickens first.

**Hazards to design.** Crumbling steps that fall a moment after you land (keep moving); beams that fall as you approach; an arena hold while the way ahead is dug out.

**Warden.** The Broken Warden. Ideas: the floor collapses section by section between phases.

**Status.** Palette only. Module: `src/zones/collapsed.js`.
