# The Forge Galleries (zone 3)

**Feel.** Heat, orange light, old machinery still ticking. Rhythm: pistons and vents fire on a beat.

**Spawn.** Constructs step out of casting molds. The mold glows as it fills; that is the tell.

**Hazards to design.** Heat vents on a timer (jump the burst); swinging hammers (duck); minecart rails as a mode section.

**Warden.** The Forge Warden. Ideas: a reheat phase where it cannot be hurt until quenched; the arena floor becomes a conveyor.

**Status.** Palette only. Module: `src/zones/forge.js`.
