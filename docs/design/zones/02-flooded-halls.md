# The Flooded Halls (zone 2)

**Feel.** Dripping, echoing, cold light from below. Water is rising behind you, which is the pressure.

**Spawn.** Shapes rise from the water: a ripple, then a silhouette, then the attack. The ripple is the tell.

**Hazards to design.** Flooded steps you must jump; a wave that sweeps the stair (duck or jump by height); a rising waterline that punishes stopping.

**Warden.** The Drowned Warden. Ideas: a pull that drags you a step down unless you jump; phase 2 raises the water and shortens the arena.

**Status.** Palette only. Uses The Deep's threats until its own exist. Module: `src/zones/flooded.js`.
