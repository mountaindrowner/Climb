# The World: the mountain in full

The plan for the mountain at three times its size: every section of it, from the bottom of the mines to the shard above
the summit, with its history, what is happening there now, who is in it, how it is lit, the rooms it is made of, and
what it is about underneath. `docs/story.md` is the direction; `docs/cast.md` is the cast; `docs/world-blueprint.md`
is the engine plan and the first sizing. This replaces that file's list of wings.

The picture is `docs/world-section.png`: the mountain cut through, with every section placed at its height.

Nothing in this file is said outright in the game. The game shows a place, and a line or two carved, written or spoken
in it. This file is so that whoever builds a room knows what happened there.

## The shape

The mountain is not only what stands up out of the land. Under the water at its roots the mines go down, level below
level, for three thousand metres, to a place no one meant to find.

The heights are the mountain's own, in Corwen's reckoning, not the game's scale: the summit stands about six thousand
metres over the water; the Source lies about three thousand under it. Corwen writes the height or the depth at the head
of each page of the Annals, and the map shows it beside each section's name.

## The history

Told nowhere in full. Pieces of it are carved, written and spoken in the sections below.

**The Age of the Flame.** A flame burned at the top of the world, and he was its keeper. Below it people made a life
round its light. A house was built on the mountain's face to keep the flame's hours, and rang them. Gardens were planted
above the cloud to feed the keepers. Pilgrims came up the Bell Road to see the light. The air above the pass was still.
At the roots the water lay clear and quiet, and people called it the Well, and did not go near it, for it was deep.

**The Night of the Fall.** He let the flame die. Why is never said. The shard broke from the summit and stayed hanging
in the air. He fell the whole height of the mountain into the water. That night the storm came, and it has not gone.

**The Long Dark.** The people did not wait. Every one of them tried to make the light come back, and every way they tried
is a section of the mountain now:

- The house rang the hours louder, and kept the rules harder, and copied the books again without a single blot.
- The Lamp-guild went down into the mountain after a pale stone that gives light when it is broken. They followed its
  vein down for three thousand metres, and built an engine to dig faster when their arms gave out.
- The Wrights built iron in the storm to catch its lightning and keep it.
- The astronomers built a brass sky that would tell them the hour the flame would come back.
- The climbers kept climbing to light it themselves. Many sat down in the cold to rest.
- The gardeners left. The garden did not.

None of it worked, and none of it stopped. The things that are still doing it are the keepers and the creatures.

**Now.** He rises out of the water at the bottom of the world.

## The undertow

Under the story there is a second thing, and it is never said either: the mountain is also what anxiety is like from
inside, and how it is answered. Every section is one shape of it. This is for the builder, so that a room has a feeling
to be built toward; it is not a lesson.

| Shape | Where | What it looks like on the mountain |
|---|---|---|
| Rumination | The Deep | Digging down after the cause, deeper and darker, by your own light, and the engine that will not stop |
| Shame, being held down | The Roots | The Warden: it will not let you rise |
| Regret, keeping count | The Ossuary | The fallen sorted and tallied |
| Compulsion, ritual | The Cloister | The hours rung for nothing |
| Perfectionism | The Scriptorium | Copy it again; one blot and begin the page over |
| The alarm that will not stop | The Bell-Foundry | The bell that was rung until it cracked |
| Comparison | The Bell Road | The names of those who went up before, at every shrine |
| The fear of falling | The Spires | Height, exposure, wind |
| Avoidance | The Pilgrims' Cairns | A stone for every one who turned back, and the reason |
| Freezing | The Glacier Galleries | The ones who sat down to rest |
| Staying where it is safe | The Last Hut | Warmth, and every reason not to go on |
| Dread | The Tempest | The storm that is always about to break |
| Control | The Wreck of the Works | Catching the lightning to keep it |
| The calm that is worse | The Eye of the Storm | Silence, waiting for it to come back |
| The bad thing in the good place | The Breach | The storm through the garden wall |
| Worry left to grow | The Gardens | What grew untended, and what was choked |
| Loneliness | The Keepers' Houses | Rooms kept ready for no one |
| Prediction | The Orrery Wing | A brass sky that says when everything will happen, and is wrong |
| The self it builds | The Firmament | The Fallen: it thinks it is him |

**The answer is never that he beats it.** Every section's answer is something given: the water gives him back, rising air
carries him, a flame was kept for him, someone swept a step before him, a light is already burning at the very bottom.
He does his part, which is to keep going. The rest is done for him. A section is finished when that has been shown, once,
quietly, and not before.

**Rules:** the words anxiety, fear, worry, panic, calm, peace and the like are never used in the game. No section
describes a feeling; it shows a place and what is being done in it. If a room would work as a poster about mental
health, cut it, as with the story.

## The sections, from the bottom

Each section below: where it is and how big; what happened there (history); what is going on there now; who is in it;
how it is lit; what it is about underneath; how he gets in; and its rooms, each named and given a line. Rooms already in
the game keep their names; new rooms are in *italic*.

---

## The Deep

Under the water. The Lamp-guild's mines, and what they broke into. Entered from the Roots by the Pithead; each level
down needs something he has learned, so the Deep is gone into four times across the game, and each time it is darker.
Its keeper is the Auger.

### The Workings
*−100 to −600 m · 14 screens · open from the start, by the Pithead off the Roots' tunnel*

**History.** The Lamp-guild's shallow mines, cut in the first years of the dark. The guild found a pale stone in the
Roots' walls that gives light when it is broken, and called it lampstone. For a while the house was lit with it. The
vein went down. So did they.

**Now.** The galleries are timbered and propped, the rails still run, the guild's lamps still hang at every turning, and
some of them still burn: someone is filling them. The Hewers are still at the faces. The cage at the shaft is stuck
halfway. Every wall is marked with a depth and a date, and the dates stop.

**Who is here.** *Hob*, the last of the guild (new person; see below), trimming lamps, counting his oil. Hewers (miners
still at their faces, who swing at anything that moves in their light), lamp-moths (drawn to his light, burst in a
flash), timber-creeps (rot in the props, which drop the roof). Elite: *the Shift-boss*, who still calls the shifts.

**Light.** Warm and small: lampstone lamps, yellow-white, pooling, with real dark between them. The first place where
the dark is something to cross.

**Underneath.** Making your own light, and how little it reaches. Hob's lamps burn down as he passes; Ordric's flames
never do.

**Rooms.** *pithead* (the winding gear, the guild's sign, the first lamp) · *the cage* (a shaft with the lift stuck in it;
he climbs the frame) · *the tally-house* (the shifts chalked on a wall, the last one half done) · *bunkhouse* (cots,
boots, a hymn scratched on a beam, unfinished) · *the long level* (a rail line across the whole section, carts, Hewers) ·
*the props* (a gallery held up by rotten timbers: it falls behind him) · *the cistern* (the bottom of the Workings, where
the water came in; see the Flooded Levels).

### The Flooded Levels
*−600 to −1,300 m · 12 screens · needs the breath (found in the cistern)*

**History.** At six hundred metres the guild cut into water. It was the same water as the pool above: it had been under
the whole mountain all along. It rose and took four levels in a night. The guild built a great pump to drive it out, and
went on digging underneath it.

**Now.** Black water to the ceiling in the low galleries, air in the high ones. The pump is still working, slowly, and the
water is still winning, slowly. Drowned carts, hanging lamps under the water still faintly lit. Things live in it.

**Who is here.** The Sunk (pale shapes that were miners, drifting under the water, that rise at him), blind eels, rafts of
rubbish that sink when stood on. Elite: *the Pump*, an iron engine that has worked for two hundred years and will not be
stopped (fought among its pistons and valves).

**Light.** Cold green under the water, from the drowned lamps; almost none above it.

**Underneath.** Being overwhelmed: the water over his head. But it is the water that gives him back. Here he learns it
holds him: he can go under, and it does not take him. Nobody says so; he finds he can breathe it.

**Rooms.** *the cistern* (shared with the Workings; where he first goes under) · *drowned level* (a whole gallery under
water, lamps still lit along it) · *air-pockets* (rooms of trapped air in a flooded level, one after the other) · *the
pump-house* (the elite) · *the overflow* (where the water pours down a stair into the dark) · *the chapel of the shift*
(a niche the miners carved, flooded to the altar; a guild lamp on it, still burning under the water).

### The Vein
*−1,300 to −2,200 m · 12 screens · needs the Warden's weight (to break the guild's seal on the deep levels)*

**History.** Below the flood the vein of lampstone grew richer and stranger: it began to glitter, to grow in crystals,
to give light on its own without being broken. The guild sealed these levels for a time, then opened them again, because
it was so bright. Here they built the Auger, when their arms could not dig fast enough.

**Now.** The tunnels follow the vein exactly, so they twist, double back, split and rejoin. The glitter is always a
little further on. Prospectors are still here, following it.

**Who is here.** Prospectors (figures with picks, faces to the glitter, who notice him only if he stands between them and
it), glimmers (crawlers that look like ore until touched), the Echo (shapes that go where he went a few seconds ago, and
strike where he was). Elite: *the Assayer*, who weighs what is brought to it and finds it light.

**Light.** The vein itself: pale blue-white, beautiful, too much; the rock beside it black.

**Underneath.** Chasing certainty: the answer always just ahead, the tunnel turning back on itself. The Echo is going over
it again.

**Rooms.** *the seal* (the guild's door, broken with the Warden's weight) · *the glitter* (the first crystal gallery) ·
*the knot* (tunnels that loop through one another; the map is needed) · *the assay* (the elite) · *the Auger's cradle*
(the hall where the engine was built, empty, its track going down into a hole) · *the lip* (the edge of the Great Shaft).

### The Great Shaft
*−2,200 to −2,900 m · 4 screens · needs the Wyrm's wind*

**History.** The Auger's hole. It bored straight down for seven hundred metres, and broke through into something.

**Now.** A drop. With the wind he can go down it; without it, it is a fall into the water and the climb again. Along its
walls the guild's last camp hangs on ropes, and their last marks: depth, date, and then only depth.

**Who is here.** Nobody. Updrafts the wrong way: cold air coming up from below, which slows him.

**Light.** None but the lamp he carries, then, at the bottom, a light that is not his.

**Underneath.** Going all the way down to the thing you are afraid of. It is the longest glide in the game and the
quietest place.

**Rooms.** *the drop* (three screens of shaft, the hanging camp in it) · *the floor* (where he lands, and sees the light).

### The Hollow
*−2,900 to −3,100 m · 14 screens · the Auger is here; past it, the Source*

**History.** Under the mountain there is a hollow nobody made, older than the house, older than the flame. The Auger
broke into its roof. The guild came down after it and were not heard from. The engine is still turning.

**Now.** A cavern too large to see across, with the Auger boring through its floor in circles, tearing it up, and the
water from above falling through the roof in long threads. In the middle of the Hollow there is a light. Nobody keeps it.
It is not lampstone.

**Who is here.** The Auger (keeper). Echoes, many. The guild, in what is left of their last camp.

**Light.** Dark, and then the one light in the middle, warm, as warm as the flames above. It is the only warm light in the
Deep, and the Deep has not prepared him for it.

**Underneath.** The bottom. The fear of what is under everything, and finding that there is a floor, and that someone was
there first. The light was never only at the top.

**Keeper: the Auger.** The guild's engine, a great drill on iron legs, still boring for a vein that ran out two hundred
years ago. Its order was *deeper*. It fights by tearing up the floor: where he stands is always about to go. Beaten by
getting onto it and breaking its bit when it bites in. It leaves him its grip: he can cling to rock and climb it.

**Rooms.** *the roof* (where the Shaft comes out, under the falling water) · *the guild's last camp* (tents, a fire long
out, the last page of the tally) · *the circles* (where the Auger has torn the floor) · *the bore* (the keeper's arena:
a floor that drops in stages as the fight goes on) · **the Source** (the end: a spring rising in the floor of the
Hollow, and beside it a flame burning low, kept by no one he can see; it lights his flask to the brim, and the water runs
up from here to the pool, to the Well, under the whole mountain). After the Source the grip opens a climb straight back up
the Hollow's wall to the Roots.

## The Roots
*0 to 500 m · 20 screens (16 now) · the start*

**History.** The foot of the mountain: caves the water made, not people. In the Age of the Flame no one came here but the
Well's keepers, who drew water and went up again. After the dark, the house set the Warden here to keep the fallen down,
and the guild cut the Pithead into the tunnel.

**Now.** Dripping dark, bats, the Warden at the top, and the water. The pool is where he wakes and where he is raised.

**Who is here.** As now (Crawler, Pale Bat, Bile-sac, Delver; the Warden). Ordric's first flame; Mirel on the bridge;
Corwen in the chamber.

**Light.** The pool's pale light, the flames, his thread.

**Underneath.** Being held down; the dark as a place you start from.

**Rooms.** start (the pool) · corridor · shaft · tunnel (*the Pithead opens off it*) · chamber · ascent · gallery · well (*the
Well's keepers' stair, carved steps worn to a curve*) · roost · bridge · grotto · arena · *the drawing-place* (new: where
the Well's keepers drew water; buckets on chains going down into the pool) · *the Ossuary door* (new: a cracked wall,
bones behind it; see the Ossuary).

### The Ossuary
*Roots, east · 9 screens · the Warden's weight*

**History.** The house laid its dead here, as near the water as it could, because it was said the water gives back. When
the dark came there were more dead than room, and the house sent sextons to sort them, and count them, and they are
counting still.

**Now.** Walls of bones sorted by kind, niches by the thousand, each with a tally scratched under it. The Tallymen
shuffle the aisles. Every fall he takes in the game is scratched on a wall here too: his own count, kept by someone else.

**Who is here.** Tallymen (bone clerks with long styluses, who mark him and then strike the mark), grave-lights. Elite:
*the Sexton*, who buries what moves.

**Light.** Candle-niches, dim gold, thousands of them, most out.

**Underneath.** Regret and keeping count. At the far end, a wall where his falls are tallied, and below the tally, in
another hand, the whole count struck through.

**Rooms.** *the door* (broken with the Warden's weight) · *the long aisle* · *the sorting* · *the niches* (a climb up a
wall of niches) · *the Sexton's yard* (the elite) · *the count* (his own tally; a relic) · *the deadhouse stair* (a
shortcut to the grotto).

### The Crypt Stair
*Seam, Roots to Cloister · 6 screens*

**History.** The house's old way down to the water, before the Warden was set: a stair cut through the rock, with the
house's founders buried along it in order, oldest lowest.

**Now.** Water runs down the stair as he goes up it. The tombs are open; the founders are gone. The stair is lit at the
top and dark at the bottom, so the Cloister's light comes down to meet him.

**Underneath.** Leaving the dark: the first time the way up is lit before he gets there.

**Rooms.** *the foot* (the Roots' look) · *the founders' stair* (the long climb, tombs in the walls) · *the light at the
top* (the Cloister's look; the first bell heard).

## The Cloister
*700 to 1,600 m · 20 screens (16 now)*

**History.** The house that kept the flame's hours. Monks rang the hours, copied the books, cast the bells, and fed the
pilgrims. When the flame went dark the house decided it had not been faithful enough, and kept everything harder: more
bells, longer hours, stricter rules, no blot on any page.

**Now.** Still ringing. The Acolytes still walk the hours. The Toll still swings.

**Who is here.** As now (Acolyte, Gargoyle, House-lamp; the Toll). Ordric's flame in the cloister, Mirel in the belfry,
Corwen in the library.

**Light.** Stained glass with nothing behind it; candles; a cold blue from the windows.

**Underneath.** Compulsion and ritual, done for a light that cannot see it.

**Rooms.** undercroft · nave · belfry · stairwell · cells · library (*the Scriptorium opens off it*) · cloister · chapel ·
*the refectory* (new: a long table set for pilgrims, bread turned to stone) · *the slype* (new: the passage to the
Bell-Foundry).

### The Scriptorium
*Cloister, west · 9 screens · the Toll's step*

**History.** Where the house's books were copied. After the dark the rule became: no page may have a single fault, or it
is burned and begun again. The copyists have been copying the same book ever since, and have never finished the first
page.

**Now.** Desks in rows, ink to the ankles, burned pages drifting in the air, a great furnace for spoiled leaves. The book
they copy is open on a lectern at the far end. Its first page is blank.

**Who is here.** Copyists (monks at desks, who fling ink and are bound to their desk), Blots (living spills that spread
where he steps). Elite: *the Corrector*, a tall figure with a knife for scraping errors.

**Light.** Candle at every desk, the furnace's red; air full of floating ash.

**Underneath.** Perfectionism: nothing is ever right enough to keep. The book's first page is blank because no one has
dared write on it. Corwen says one line about it and nothing more.

**Rooms.** *the stacks of spoils* · *the copying hall* (rows of desks; climb across them) · *the furnace* · *the
rubrication* (a red room; the elite) · *the lectern* (the blank book; a relic) · *the chute* (a shortcut down the paper
chute to the undercroft).

### The Bell-Foundry
*Cloister, east · 9 screens · the Warden's weight*

**History.** Where the house cast its bells, in a pit cut into the mountain. They cast the Toll here. When the dark came
they cast more and larger, and rang them harder, until one cracked: a sister bell to the Toll. They left it in the pit
and went on casting.

**Now.** Molds, slag, a cold furnace, and the cracked bell hanging in the pit, still ringing when anything touches it,
a broken note that shakes the room.

**Who is here.** Slag-crawlers, Clappers (iron tongues of broken bells, which swing). Mini-keeper: *the Cracked Bell*:
every hit it takes rings through the whole section, waking everything.

**Light.** Orange from the slag, black iron.

**Underneath.** The alarm that will not stop, rung until it broke.

**Rooms.** *the casting floor* · *the molds* · *the slag runs* · *the pit* (the Cracked Bell) · *the bell-rope climb*
(a shortcut up to the belfry).

### The Bell Road
*Seam, Cloister to Spires · 8 screens*

**History.** The pilgrims' road from the house up to the pass, with a shrine at every turn. At each shrine the pilgrims
carved their names, so that those behind them would know who had gone ahead.

**Now.** The road climbs out of the house's gate into the open. Thousands of names on every shrine. Grey-threaded pilgrims
still walk it (not hostile): some going up, some sitting, a few coming down.

**Who is here.** Pilgrims. Mirel, on the road, counting names.

**Underneath.** Comparison: everyone who went before, all of them further on. His name is on none of them.

**Rooms.** *the gate* (the Cloister's look) · *the road* (a long climb, shrines at the turns) · *the shrine of names* (a
landmark) · *the last shrine* (no names on it; the Spires' look).

## The Spires
*1,800 to 2,800 m · 22 screens (20 now)*

**History.** The mountain's bones above the house: the pass, ice, sky. In the Age of the Flame it was the pilgrims' last
stage. The Wyrm coiled on the pass the night of the fall and has let no one by.

**Now.** As now. The cold, the hawks, the Stopped.

**Who is here.** As now (Rimeback, High Hawk, the Stopped; the Rime Wyrm). Ordric in the hollow, Mirel in the eyrie,
Corwen on the saddle.

**Light.** Hard white sun, blue shadow.

**Underneath.** The fear of falling: height and exposure.

**Rooms.** mouth · saddle · nest · spire · hollow · ropeway · chimney · icecave (*the Glacier Galleries open off it*) ·
teeth · eyrie · span · crown · *the cairn-field* (new: the way to the Cairns).

### The Pilgrims' Cairns
*Spires, west · 9 screens · the Wyrm's wind*

**History.** Where pilgrims who turned back built a cairn before they went down, each stone a reason.

**Now.** Thousands of cairns on a high slope in the wind, some fallen, some still being built. Words scratched on the
stones: *too cold*, *not yet*, *not me*.

**Who is here.** Cairns that come apart into stone creatures when he passes. Elite: *the Turned*, a climber standing
facing downhill, who will not let anyone go up past him.

**Light.** Wind-scoured, bright, the sky huge.

**Underneath.** Avoidance, with good reasons. Among the cairns there is one with his name on it, from before he fell. He
can knock it down.

**Rooms.** *the wind gap* · *the cairn-field* · *the reasons* (the stones' words) · *the Turned* · *his cairn* · *the
scree run* (a shortcut down to the mouth).

### The Glacier Galleries
*Spires, east · 12 screens · the Toll's step*

**History.** Caves in the glacier where climbers sheltered from the wind and sat down to rest. The ice took them slowly.
The Stopped are from here.

**Now.** Blue galleries, and in the walls, climbers in the ice, sitting, eyes shut. Some of them have come loose and
drift.

**Who is here.** The Stopped (many), ice. Elite: *the Seated*, a giant figure in the ice, half-freed.

**Light.** Blue through ice, very dim.

**Underneath.** Freezing: the rest that becomes stopping. Mirel sits here once, and gets up.

**Rooms.** *the shelter* · *the sitters* · *the deep ice* · *the Seated* · *the meltwater* (a stream running out; a shortcut).

### The Last Hut
*Seam, Spires to Tempest · 6 screens*

**History.** The climbers' last shelter below the storm: a stone hut with a fire, built when the storm was new and the
climbers still meant to go through it.

**Now.** Warm. A fire Ordric keeps, a pot, blankets, beds. The storm through the window. Everything about it says stay.

**Who is here.** Ordric; sometimes all three. No creatures.

**Underneath.** Staying where it is safe. He has to leave it, and the door is heavy.

**Rooms.** *the path up* (the Spires' look) · *the hut* · *the door* (opening it, the Tempest's wind comes in).

## The Tempest
*3,000 to 4,000 m · 21 screens (19 now)*

**History.** There was no storm above the pass while the flame burned. It came the night of the fall and has not moved.
The Wrights came after, to catch its lightning.

**Now.** As now: rigging, masts, rods, rising air, the Thunderhead.

**Who is here.** As now (Squall Knight, Nimbus, Ball Lightning; the Thunderhead). Ordric at the masthead, Mirel at the
gantry (her pack left behind: she cannot carry it here), Corwen in the stacks.

**Light.** Grey-violet, lightning-white, sudden.

**Underneath.** Dread: the storm always about to break. And the answer is in the storm itself: the rising air carries him.

**Rooms.** landing · rigging · anchorage · stacks · watch · gantry (*the Wreck opens off it*) · flue · crossing ·
masthead (*the Eye's way opens above it*) · gale.

### The Wreck of the Works
*Tempest, west · 12 screens · open*

**History.** The Wrights built a works on the storm's edge to catch the lightning in jars and coils and keep it, and so
make their own flame. The storm took it apart.

**Now.** A wreck of iron spread across the cliff, half of it still sparking, coils still drawing lightning, the great jar
cracked and humming.

**Who is here.** Coils (sparking machines), Wright's-arms (loose cranes that swing). Elite: *the Jar*, the great jar of
caught lightning, which lets it go all at once.

**Light.** Blue sparks, white bolts drawn down into the iron.

**Underneath.** Control: keeping the thing that frightens you in a jar.

**Rooms.** *the yard* · *the coils* · *the cranes* · *the jar-house* (the elite) · *the drafting room* (the Wrights' plans;
a relic) · *the cable run* (a shortcut along a cable to the anchorage).

### The Eye of the Storm
*Tempest, east, above · 9 screens · the Wyrm's wind*

**History.** The middle of the storm, where it is still. The Thunderhead sat here before it came down to fight.

**Now.** Silence. Clear sky, sun, no wind, after all that noise. An empty seat of cloud. Things move only when he stops.

**Who is here.** The Hush (shapes that move only while he stands still). No elite: the fight is the waiting.

**Light.** Clear, pale, too bright.

**Underneath.** The calm that is worse. He must go through it moving.

**Rooms.** *the wall* (the storm's wall, gone through on the wind) · *the still* · *the seat* · *the way out* (falling
back into the storm; a shortcut to the gale).

### The Breach
*Seam, Tempest to Gardens · 8 screens*

**History.** The garden wall, built against the cold, where the storm broke through the night it came.

**Now.** The storm half in and half out of the garden: torn trellis, drowned beds, rain on flowers, a gate hanging. Above
the breach, sun.

**Who is here.** Storm and garden both: Nimbus over rose beds, Lashers in the rain.

**Underneath.** The bad thing in the good place, and the good place still there.

**Rooms.** *the wall* (the Tempest's look) · *the breach* · *the drowned beds* · *the stormdoor* (the Gardens' look).

## The Gardens
*4,200 to 5,000 m · 19 screens (16 now)*

**History.** Planted above the cloud to feed the keepers. The gardeners left after the dark. Nobody told the garden.

**Now.** As now: overgrown, beautiful, choking, Mother Thorn at the top.

**Who is here.** As now (Thornback, Lasher, Rot-pod; Mother Thorn). Ordric in the orangery, Mirel at the cascade (she has
put the pack down and does not know what to do with her hands), Corwen on the terraces.

**Light.** Sun, warm, the first gold light since the Roots' flames.

**Underneath.** Worry left to grow; and rest.

**Rooms.** stormdoor · terraces · wellspring (*the Aqueducts open below it*) · brambles · arbor · orangery · cascade ·
sundial · canopy · bower · *the gardeners' gate* (new: the way to the Keepers' Houses).

### The Aqueducts
*Gardens, west and below · 9 screens · open*

**History.** The garden's water came from the pool at the bottom of the world: carried up by the Well's keepers in the
first days, then by a channel through the mountain that the house built. It still runs.

**Now.** Stone channels, sluices, arches over the cloud, the water still flowing up through the mountain and out into the
garden. Machines keep the sluices.

**Who is here.** Sluice-keepers (stone automata that open and shut the gates, and shut them on him), water-striders.
Elite: *the Lock*, a great gate that fights as it opens.

**Light.** Sun on water; cool shade under the arches.

**Underneath.** The water reaches even here. The same water. Nobody up here remembers where it comes from.

**Rooms.** *the head* (where the water comes out of the rock) · *the arches* · *the sluices* · *the lock* · *the spill* (a
shortcut to the wellspring).

### The Keepers' Houses
*Gardens, east · 9 screens · the Toll's step*

**History.** Where the keepers of the flame lived: his house among them. They left after the dark. The house's servants
did not.

**Now.** Small houses in a row, every one kept: beds made, tables laid, fires laid and not lit. An automaton goes from
house to house setting the tables for people who are not coming. One house is his.

**Who is here.** *The Housekeeper* (elite; attacks what disarranges the rooms), moths.

**Light.** Afternoon sun through shutters; dust in it.

**Underneath.** Loneliness: rooms kept ready for no one. In his house the table is laid for two.

**Rooms.** *the lane* · *the houses* (three, each a small climb inside) · *the Housekeeper's round* · *his house* (a relic;
the table laid for two; nothing said) · *the back way* (a shortcut to the bower).

### The Long Stair
*Seam, Gardens to Firmament · 8 screens*

**History.** The keepers' stair to the summit, a step for every day of the year, swept every morning.

**Now.** A stair up into the stars, swept. Someone is still sweeping it: a figure always a few steps ahead of him, who
is never caught up with and never turns round.

**Who is here.** The Sweeper (not a creature; not a person met). Nothing hostile.

**Underneath.** Endurance; and that someone went before him and made the way clean.

**Rooms.** *the foot* (the Gardens' look) · *the stair* (long, quiet, the sky darkening into stars) · *the top step* (the
Firmament's look; the broom leaning against the wall).

## The Firmament
*5,200 to 6,000 m · 21 screens (19 now)*

**History.** The summit, where the flame burned and he kept it. After the dark the astronomers came up to measure the sky
and work out when the flame would come back.

**Now.** As now: star-glass, brass, the Fallen at the top.

**Who is here.** As now (Sentinel, Oculus, Star-cinder; the Fallen). Ordric at the vigil, Mirel at the transit (she waits,
unhurried), Corwen in the archive.

**Light.** Starlight, close; brass gold; the dark sky.

**Underneath.** The self it builds: the Fallen thinks it is him.

**Rooms.** threshold · terrace · meridian · orrery (*the Orrery Wing opens off it*) · gnomon · archive · lens · transit ·
ascension · vigil · aphelion · zenith.

### The Orrery Wing
*Firmament, west · 9 screens · open*

**History.** The astronomers' great work: a brass sky, every star on an arm, wound to run forward, to tell the hour the
flame would come back. It has told it many times.

**Now.** Brass arms turning through the rooms, planets on them, the whole wing moving; he rides it. The predicted hours are
engraved on the floor, hundreds of them, all past.

**Who is here.** Armillaries (rings that roll), the Almanac (elite: an automaton that reads the hour and strikes there).

**Light.** Brass in starlight; the mechanism's glow.

**Underneath.** Prediction: if it can be worked out it can be survived. The dates on the floor are all wrong.

**Rooms.** *the escapement* · *the arms* (riding the orrery) · *the hours* (the floor of dates) · *the Almanac* · *the
winding room* (a shortcut).

### The Shard
*Above the summit · 6 screens · everything he has learned, and the grip*

**History.** It broke from the top the night he fell and has not come down.

**Now.** Unexplained, and it stays so. Reached by climbing the summit's underside with the grip, and gliding out over
nothing to it. It is a piece of the mountain hanging in the air with a little of the old light still in it.

**Who is here.** No one.

**Underneath.** Left alone on purpose.

**Rooms.** *the underside* · *the gap* · *the shard*.

## The people, through the whole mountain

The three keep their stations in the six regions (`docs/cast.md`) and appear once more each in a section that suits them:
Ordric in the Last Hut, Mirel on the Bell Road and sitting down in the Glacier Galleries, Corwen at the Source if he gets
there (he does not, and his last page has the depth of it written, and nothing else).

**New: Hob, the last of the Lamp-guild.** Met in the Workings, the Flooded Levels and the Vein, always further down. Old,
blackened, a little lamp on his hat. He fills the guild's lamps and counts his oil, and is sure the vein will come good
lower down. He is the Deep's answer to Ordric: Ordric keeps flames he was given; Hob makes lights that burn out. He does
not climb. Met for the last time at the Source, sitting by the flame no one keeps, with his lamp out, not needing it.

## New things he learns

The three he has open most of the mountain at double size. At three times it needs two more, and both come from the
Deep:

| Learned | Where | What it does | What it opens |
|---|---|---|---|
| The lamp | Ordric gives it, at the Roots' first flame after the Warden | A small light he carries | The Deep below the Workings is too dark to cross without it |
| The breath | The cistern (Flooded Levels) | He can go under the water | The Flooded Levels; drowned rooms in the Ossuary and the Aqueducts |
| The Warden's weight | The Warden | Breaks stone (as now) | The Ossuary, the Bell-Foundry, the Vein's seal |
| The Toll's step | The Toll | Dash in the air (as now) | The Scriptorium, the Glacier Galleries, the Keepers' Houses |
| The Wyrm's wind | The Rime Wyrm | Glide (as now) | The Cairns, the Eye, the Great Shaft |
| The grip | The Auger | Cling to rock and climb it | The Shard; the climb out of the Hollow; high ledges throughout |

The lamp is given, not found, and it does not burn down: the guild's lamps do.

## The order of play this makes

He climbs; the mountain keeps sending him down. A first run of the climb sees the Workings, the Bell Road, the Last Hut,
the Breach, the Long Stair and the open wings. Each keeper's gift opens something already passed. The Deep is gone into
four times: once at the start (the Workings), once for the breath, once after the Warden for the Vein, and once after the
Wyrm for the Shaft and the Hollow. The Source can be found before the Fallen or after; the ending reads differently
either way, and should never need it.

## Building it

The engine work comes first, as in `docs/world-blueprint.md` (painting by room, sleeping far creatures, a scrolling map).
Then, in this order, so that each step is playable:

1. **The Workings** by hand: the first new section, the first dark, Hob. It proves a section's size and feel and how long
   one takes.
2. **The seams**: short, mostly scenery, they make the regions one mountain at once.
3. **The wings**, two regions at a time, with the chunk library drawn from what the Workings taught.
4. **The rest of the Deep**, the breath and the grip, the Auger and the Source.
5. **The Shard** last.

Every section, before it is built, gets a sheet: its rooms in order, what each one is for (a fight, a climb, a look, a
rest), its gate and shortcut, its creatures, its light, and its one moment.
