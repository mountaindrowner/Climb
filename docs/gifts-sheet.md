## The proposal

### 1. Weapons from keepers

Every keeper that falls leaves a weapon, and each weapon is complete: its own combo, its own strength, its own
weakness (decision 0.4). He carries them all and swaps between them.

| Keeper | Leaves | What it is | Strong | Weak |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (he starts with) | The curved blade | Fast three-hit combo; it blocks and parries | Speed, defence | Cannot break stone; short |
| The Warden | **The hammer** (in place of the spade) | A sledge. Two heavy blows and a ground slam with a shockwave | Much more damage a hit; a wide arc; **knocks things back**; breaks cracked stone | Slow; cannot block; weak in the air |
| The Toll | **The clapper** | The bell's tongue on its rope: a flail | The longest reach; a hit rings, and stops small creatures for a breath | Slow to recover; nothing up close |
| The Wyrm | **The tooth** | A spear of its ice | Long thrusts; **strikes downward in the air and bounces him off what it hits** | Narrow: it must be aimed |
| The Thunderhead | **The fork** | A rod that throws a bolt | The only weapon that reaches across a room | Each throw spends embers; weak at arm's length |
| Mother Thorn | **The thorns** | A pair of short blades | The fastest; hits build to a burst | The shortest reach; no block |

That is six. **I would build them one at a time**, the hammer first, each on its own sheet with its drawings shown to
you before they are animated. You have about 9,000 generations, so the art is affordable; each weapon is five or six
attack drawings for him.

### 2. Abilities from minibosses

The abilities move from the keepers to the wings' minibosses. Fitted to how the wings are gated today, it falls out
cleanly, and it makes each region what you asked for: a main way, and a wing either side that matters.

| Ability | Today from | Would come from | Why there |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The air dash** | The Toll | **The Cracked Bell** (the Bell-Foundry) | It is a bell: "the Toll's step" becomes the Foundry's. The Foundry is behind cracked stone, so it needs the Warden's hammer: one gift leads to the next |
| **The glide** | The Wyrm | **The Seated** (the Glacier Galleries) | The Galleries are behind the air dash. Again one leads to the next |
| **The grip** | The Auger | The Auger, as now | The Deep is shut. Settled when it opens |

**What follows from that**

- **Two wings become part of the climb.** He cannot enter the Spires' heights without the dash, so he must go
  through the Foundry. He cannot cross the Tempest without the glide, so he must go through the Galleries. That is
  the "far left, far right, and the main way" you described (0.19), and it is why the regions will feel longer.
- **The Cracked Bell has to become a real fight.** You found it confusing and passive. It would be rebuilt (your
  idea: a broken, rejected bell that rolls) before it is given something this important.
- **The heart and the drink** the Thunderhead and Mother Thorn give today go to a miniboss in each of their regions.
- **Every miniboss leaves its prize in a chest of its own kind,** larger and marked, that opens only when it falls.
  The strongest relics (you named the Sexton's and the Climber's Knot) move behind them.

### 3. How hard: three to five deaths a region

Today it is none. Four things make it easy, and I would change them in this order, measuring after each. The game
already counts deaths by region, so each playtest gives a number to steer by.

| What | Today | Proposed | Why first or last |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Springs** (heal, refill the flask) | Two or three a region, besides the checkpoint pool | **One a region**, in the middle | You asked for it. Cheap, and it makes the flask matter |
| **Money** | You owned everything Ordric sells by the Tempest | **Prices up by about two thirds,** and fewer shards from urns, so that a region buys one thing | Cheap. I will measure what a region pays first and show you the numbers |
| **What a hit costs** | Almost everything takes one heart | The larger creatures and every keeper's big blow **take two** | Cheap. It is what makes five hearts feel like five |
| **The keepers** | Beaten the same way, first try | Each made a fight of its own, **the Wyrm first** (your notes on it are the longest) | Slow: one keeper a build |

**One thing only you can decide:** does dying cost anything? Today he rises at the pool with everything he had. Your
brother's idea was that what he carried stays where he fell. I have left it out until you say.

## In what order

1. **The hammer** in place of the spade: its sheet, its drawings, then the build.
2. **The gifts moved:** the dash to the Cracked Bell (rebuilt), the glide to the Seated, the heart and the drink to
   minibosses, the special chest.
3. **Springs, money, and two-heart blows.** One build, then your death counts.
4. **The Wyrm rebuilt.** Then the other keepers, one a build.
5. **The second weapon,** and the rest, one a build.

Steps 1 to 3 change what every later region is built on, so they come before the map pass goes on to the Cloister.
