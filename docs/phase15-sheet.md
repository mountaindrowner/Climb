# Phase 15: the lamp, and the Vein (the sheet)

*The plan was written before building; "As built" at the end says what came of it.*

Two things, and the second needs the first: a light he carries, and the first part of the Deep that is truly dark.

## The lamp

Ordric gives it after the Warden. It is a small light he carries; it does not burn down. It matters only where it is
dark, and until now nowhere was.

- **Where it is dark** (the Vein, and the Deep below it later), he sees by the lamp: a pool of light round him, and
  nothing past it but what gives its own light.
- **The guild's lamps** hang unlit through the dark levels. Coming up to one with the lamp lights it, and **it stays
  lit**, for good: it is in his save. So the dark is something he pushes back as he goes, and the way he came is
  lit behind him.
- **A lit lamp is safe ground from the Echo** (below). That is what makes lighting them more than a comfort.
- **How it is given:** the plan says "at the Roots' first flame after the Warden". Built as: he has it from the moment
  the Warden is down (a save that is already past the Warden has it at once), and the next time he comes to any
  raising water he is told Ordric left it there for him. It cannot be missed, and nobody reaches the dark without it.

## The Vein

*The Deep, under the Workings. Twelve screens. Behind the guild's seal, which the Warden's spade breaks.*

Below the flood the lampstone grew richer and stranger: it began to glitter, to grow in crystals, to give light without
being broken. The guild sealed these levels, then opened them again, because it was so bright. The tunnels follow the
vein exactly, so they twist and double back. The glitter is always a little further on.

(The world plan puts the Flooded Levels between the Workings and the Vein. The roadmap builds the Vein first, so for
now the seal is at the Workings' cistern and its stair goes down past the flood. When the Flooded Levels are built the
seal can move, or stay as the dry way down.)

### Its rule: do not go back over it

- **It is dark.** His lamp, the lamps he has lit, and the vein's own crystals are all the light there is.
- **The Echo** goes where he went a few seconds ago, and strikes where he was. It cannot be struck. Standing still
  lets it catch him; so does doubling back along his own way. **It will not come into the light of a lit guild
  lamp:** there it waits at the edge. So every lamp he lights is a place to stop.
- **Prospectors** face the glitter and notice him only if he stands between them and it. Behind them he is nothing.
- **Glimmers** look like ore until he is on them.

### How it is entered, and left

- **In:** a new room off the foot of the Workings' cistern: the guild's seal (cracked stone: the Warden's spade), two
  lamps to light on the way (the lesson, in a place that is not yet dark), and a stair down.
- **Out:** the same stair. The Vein has its own raising water at its head, so a fall there is not a long walk back.

### The rooms

1. **The seal** (one screen). The foot of the stair, the water, the first dark.
2. **The glitter** (two screens: the vast space). The first crystal gallery, bright. Prospectors at the faces, their
   backs to him. Glimmers among the real crystals.
3. **The knot** (four screens: the winding way). Three galleries one over another, joined by shafts, looping. An Echo.
   A lamp at every joining. A thread.
4. **The assay** (two screens). The Vein's keeper.
5. **The Auger's cradle** (two screens). The hall where the engine was built, empty, its track going down into a hole.
   The relic.
6. **The lip** (one screen: the path no one expects). The edge of the Great Shaft. The way on, later. A thread.

### What is in it

- **Prospector.** Four blows. A pick, a long reach, a clear wind-up.
- **Glimmer.** Two blows. Fast once woken.
- **The Echo.** Not to be struck. One in the knot, one in the cradle; a third comes when the Assayer is nearly done.
- **The Assayer** (the Vein's keeper; about 26 blows). A great balance: it weighs what is brought to it and finds it
  light. It does not move. Its beam tips toward the side he stands on, and that pan comes down. Later both pans, one
  after the other, so the only safe places are under the post and out past the pans.

### The relic: Raw Lampstone

*A piece of the vein that gives light without being broken. The guild sealed the level it came from, and then opened
it again.* **His lamp reaches further.**

### Art

The Prospector (a swing, a walk) and the glimmer are PixelLab's. The Echo, the Assayer, the lamps and the crystals'
light are drawn in code. **Cost: $0.12; the balance is $2.42.**

## As built (build 72), and what was checked

- **338 automated checks pass**, nine of them this phase's own: the Vein's screens, its dark, its water, stair and
  lamps; the stair out taken by a jump from its rungs; the Workings' seal, and the stair behind it reached only with
  the spade; the Prospector; the glimmer; the Echo catching one who stands and not one who goes on; the Echo held at
  the edge of a lit lamp's light, and not by an unlit one; the Assayer; the relic and the count of things to find.
- **Played through by the test browser:** a guild lamp lit by coming to it; three seconds behind a Prospector with
  nothing, then between him and the glitter and struck; a glimmer still at four tiles and biting at one; the Echo
  catching him after he walked and stood in the dark; the same walk to a lamp, lit, and six seconds under it with
  the Echo held at the edge; ten seconds under one of the Assayer's pans (four blows) and ten under its post
  (none); the Assayer broken, its hatch open, down into the cradle; the relic; out onto the lip; up the stair into
  the Workings behind the seal, and back down the hole into the Vein.
- **Found and fixed while building:**
  - **The way out of the Vein could not be taken.** I had set it at the top of the stair, but that room's roof is
    the top of the level, and he cannot jump into the last rows under it. The checks passed it (they ask only that
    he can get near). Found by playing it; the way up is now taken from the third rung, and a check plays the jump.
  - The dark was too dark to play in: his lamp's pool was faint and small. The lamp, the lit lamps and the crystals
    are brighter now.
  - A lit lamp's glow was drawn as a hard square.
  - A lit lamp's safe ground was smaller than its light looked, so standing in the light was not safe. It is wider.
  - The wear had banked rock against the guild's seal, which would have shut the way under the wall even with the
    seal broken. The floor is held level there.
  - The older checks took the new stair for the Workings' way out, and said a passage behind a gate "cannot be
    reached". They are taught: a gated passage must be reached with its gate open, and not before.
  - Each level of the Deep has Hob in it once, and the Vein had no Hob. He is at its water, under the second lamp,
    with three things to say; the third is the Echo's rule.
  - **Ordric had been drawn wrong since build 69** (my fault: see the build log). Mended in this build.
- **Not as the plan had it:**
  - The lamp is not handed over in a scene. He has it from the Warden on, and is told at the next water.
  - The seal is at the Workings' cistern, not below the Flooded Levels, which are not built.
  - The Prospectors do not test whether he is in the light: only whether he is between them and their crystal.
  - The knot is three straight galleries joined by four shafts. It loops, but it is not hard to hold in the head, and
    Corwen's map is not needed for it.
- **Not checked:** nothing here was played by hand. The sounds. **How dark it really looks on a screen**: I have only
  my own captures, and darkness is the thing most likely to be wrong on someone else's display. Whether the Echo is
  frightening or only a nuisance. Whether the Assayer is too easy once the post is found to be safe (it always is).
- **A save is not moved.** A save that is already past the Warden has the lamp at once.
