# The words pass (the sheet)

*Asked for after the playtest of 20 Nov 2026: "we're too vague and ambiguous, too poetic... we have to speak to the
player himself. We have to instruct, teach, direct." Build 77. Every changed line is in the table at the end.*

## What was wrong

Two people played and could not say what a relic did, what "a measure" was, where to go, or why. The game's own
writing guide (`docs/writing.md`) was built for the story: give half, never say the theme, speak like an inscription.
It was being applied to instructions as well. A relic read *Once each rest, an Echo that catches him does not hurt
him.* That is a rule of play written as a line of scripture.

## The rule: two voices

Every line is now one of two kinds (`docs/writing.md`, "Two voices").

1. **Player text** is anything the player acts on: hints, cards, abilities, relics' effects, the shop, menus, the
   map, goals, how a keeper is hurt. It says **you**. It says what to do or what a thing does. It uses the player's
   words and one word for one thing. It gives numbers. It names what is needed. It carries no flavour.
2. **World text** is what the place and its people say. It can keep its gravity. It may never be the only place
   something needed is said. A person's first two lines in a region are useful; the third is their own.

## The goal, said plainly

- **The intro** now says who you are, what happened, and what to do: *You kept the flame at the top of this mountain.
  You let it go out. You fell.* ... *The water brought you back. Far above, a light is calling you. Climb back to the
  top.* The ending is in the same voice: *The flame you let die was burning. It had never gone out. It had gone down
  into the dark to find you.*
- **The pause screen** opens with two lines: the goal of the game, and the next step from where you are.
  *GOAL: CLIMB TO THE FLAME AT THE TOP OF THE MOUNTAIN. NEXT: FIND THE WARDEN AT THE TOP OF THE ROOTS. IT BLOCKS THE
  WAY UP.* The second line changes when the keeper is beaten, and in the Deep it says the Deep is optional.
- **The Deep is marked as off the climb.** Standing at a way down into it: *This goes to the Deep, under the
  mountain. It is optional, and harder. The climb is the other way: up.* The first time in it, the same.
- **Each person points the way.** See below.
- **A blocked way says what opens it.** On the map: *BLOCKED BY CRACKED STONE: NEEDS A HEAVY WEAPON.*
- **Deep water says what it needs:** *You float, but you cannot dive yet. You need the Wyrm's wind.*

## Less text when a thing is given

- **An ability's card has two lines of how.** The Warden's Spade had four. The other two (its third hit; its ember
  power) are in the Inventory, with everything else about it.
- **A relic's card says what it does and whether it is equipped.** Its history is in the Inventory, not on the card.
- **Banners wrap.** A long line used to run off both sides of the screen. It now wraps and sits on a dark band.

## What this pass did not do

- **The Annals** (the lore book in the pause menu) are untouched. They are world text and optional.
- **Relics' histories** are untouched, apart from "he" where it meant the player.
- **A second typeface.** "Everything's the same font" is true and is not fixed here.
- **A taught walk-through of relics and embers** by the first person met. The cards are plainer; they are still cards.
- **An inventory of what could be found.** The Inventory shows what you have.
- **Whether the Deep should be shut until later.** It is only labelled. Shutting it is a change to the world.
