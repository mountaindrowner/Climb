## The rules (yours)

- **Flat.** Every creature is drawn from the side, in two dimensions. Never at an angle, never on a diagonal, never
  facing out of the screen.
- **No plain humans.**
- **Four kinds in every region:** a ground fighter, a ground shooter, a flier that touches, a flier that shoots.
- **Placed on purpose,** not cluttered.
- **The standard** is the red crawler.
- **Made without asking for each** (26 Nov). I still show you each batch when it is in the game.

## The way to draw them: found (27 Nov)

PixelLab's **character** tool draws things that stand up, so a bat came out as a standing figure. Its **picture**
tool, asked for a side view on a clear ground, draws a creature flat and in profile. Three tries at the Drowned
Galleries' skimmer all came out side-on (`docs/skimmer-candidates.png`: the crawler and the water-strider as they
are in the game, then tries a, b and c). One generation each.

What it does not give is movement: a picture is one frame. For a flier, a second frame with the wings down is
enough, and I can make that from the first. A walker needs more, and that is still to be worked out.

**The Squid** (build 111) was drawn the same way, at the size it is in the game; its arms' four frames are that one
drawing bent. (For one build, 110, it was a pike. Your word: not a fish.) **A rule from that:** what lives in the
mountain's water is a thing of the deep, not a fish.

**Drawing b is in the game** (build 109), on your word of 27 Nov ("They all look great"). Its four wing frames
are that one drawing bent.

## How each is made

1. **One standing drawing,** from the side, at the size it has in the game. Looked at by me beside the crawler: if it
   is not flat it is made again.
2. **Its moves** from that one drawing (walk or fly, the tell, the blow, hurt, death), so it is the same creature in
   every frame.
3. **Into the game** in place of the old drawing, at the same size on screen unless a note says otherwise.
4. **Checked:** it stands on something, nothing begins in rock, the fight's timing is as it was.
5. **Shown to you** as a strip beside the old one.

## The order

1. **The redraws, a region a build,** from the Roots up: same creature, same fight, flat.
2. **The six new creatures,** a region a build.
3. **The notes that change how a creature fights** (C1, C3, C7, C8, C13, C14), with that region's build.
4. **Placing:** a rule for how many and where, tried in the Roots first.

**Cost:** PixelLab has about 7,900 generations left this month; a creature with five moves has cost 5 to 8. The
whole pass is well inside that.

## What I cannot judge

Whether a drawing is good. I can tell flat from angled and the same creature from a different one; whether you like
it is yours. Each batch comes to you as a strip.
