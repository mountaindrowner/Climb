# 0009 — The Warden: a colossus in a cavern where the stair ends

**Context.** The Warden fight was parked (0006) as a running fight from behind the wall, designed for the old auto-climb. Since then the warrior moves freely (0007), and the concept listed two arena types for milestone 7: the Ring and the Colossus. The designer supplied a stone golem modelled and rigged in Tripo and asked first for the boss "at the top of a level: the floor ends and you have to fight him", then for "a massive space in the level where the golem looms massive in the background and his arms reach to the stage and try to smash the player".

**Decision.** The Colossus comes first. At the top of every region the stair ends in a broad flat floor (the region's last flight, made flat; its rise moves to the region's other flat flight, so no region changes height) with a sheer edge where the steps above have fallen away. Over the floor the shaft wall, and the shell behind it, are cut away into a window onto a vast cavern, and the golem stands in its depths, forty units tall, his torso above the floor. Stepping onto the floor brings the stair down behind you in rubble. He never comes onto the floor: his arms reach to it, by two-bone IK on the model's own bones every frame, so a blow lands exactly where the game aims it. The fight: a slam with a shadow tell, then the fist resting on the floor (the opening); a sweep at chest height (crouch); poise on his fists that makes him reel and lower his head to the floor for double damage; a second phase with no prompts, both fists, shockwaves and rocks. Beaten, he topples back into the dark and the first flight of the region above rises into place; the win is kept, and a lift from the Pool runs past him. A first version (0.27 in development) had him the same size as a big creature, fighting face to face on the floor; the designer asked for the colossus instead before it shipped.

**Consequences.** Every region has the same Warden, stronger with shorter wind-ups each region, until each has its own. Nothing else is placed on the floor (no threats, ledges, oath stones, halls, or switchbacks coming up past it), which moved one region's hidden hall to a fallback landing. The camera holds the floor and him together with a wider view; it cannot back further away without backing into the stair on the near side of the shaft. Every scene light costs every material, so the fight adds only one light. The test bot fights him on the full climb and must win to reach the second region. The parked running fight stays behind its switch; with it on, the old layout returns and there are no floors.
