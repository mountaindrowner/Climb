# 0009 — The Warden waits on a floor where the stair ends

**Context.** The Warden fight was parked (0006) as a running fight from behind the wall, designed for the old auto-climb. Since then the warrior moves freely (0007) and fights creatures face to face, and the concept listed two arena types for milestone 7. The designer then supplied a stone golem modelled and rigged in Tripo and asked for the boss "at the top of a level: the floor ends and you have to fight him".

**Decision.** At the top of every region the stair ends in a flat floor (the region's last flight, made flat; its rise moves to the region's other flat flight, so no region changes height). The golem waits there; stepping onto the floor brings the stair down behind you in rubble, and the far end is a sheer edge where the steps above have fallen away. The fight is face to face on the lane, readable Dead Cells style: each attack has its own answer (out of reach of the slam, under the sweep, over the shockwave, out from under the leap), a long opening after the slam, poise that knocks him onto one knee, a parry that does it at once, and a second phase that drops the prompts and brings rocks down from the roof. Beaten, he crumbles and the first flight of the region above rises into place; the win is kept, and a lift from the Pool runs past him. This is the Ring arena type in all but its shape: the lane is a stretch of floor rather than a full circle. The parked running fight stays behind its switch; with it on, the old layout returns.

**Consequences.** Every region now has a Warden at its top, the same golem with more strength and shorter wind-ups each region until each region has its own. Nothing else is placed on the floor (no threats, ledges, oath stones, halls, or switchbacks coming up past it), which moved one region's hidden hall to a fallback landing. The test bot fights him on the full climb and must win to reach the second region.
