# 0010 — Movement v2: Dead Cells' controls, as near one for one as their published sources allow

**Context.** The designer brought research on what makes Dead Cells' movement feel the way it does (a source dossier and a reference controller) and asked for it as close to one for one as possible, with our own flair. The research is careful about provenance: the developer's GDC 2019 talk confirms the techniques (just-in-time jumps, arrival assistance, a double jump from the start, free turnaround, auto-aim), and his MIT-licensed game-feel demo (`deepnight/gamefeel`, read for this decision) shows how he builds them (an input queue, gravity eased in after take-off, a stronger double jump, air control that fades, exponential braking, landing effects by height, step and cliff helpers). The shipped game's exact numbers are not public. Since 0.17 (decision 0007) the motor already had most of the base: instant turning, air control, a variable jump, coyote time, a jump buffer, the wiki's roll timing, combo buffering, the ledge grab.

**Decision.** Bring the motor to Dead Cells' full set, tuned to our warrior's size rather than copied numbers: an input queue for every action; a double jump from the start (with a somersault); a launch whose gravity eases in, so the jump pops then floats, and the held jump keeps its height; air control a touch stronger than on the ground, fading over a long fall; a fast run (5.5) and no sprint button; free turning early in a swing and a little aim at a foe behind; a roll that cancels anything, cut short into an attack or a jump near its end; a plunge from the air (down + attack) with a slam either side; heavy landings only from real drops. A feel panel (F9) switches each assist on and off, as the developer's demo does. The platforming is re-tuned around the double jump (wider gaps, higher ledges).

**Consequences.** Shift is the roll's first key now (sprint is gone, and its binding with it; saved controls drop it). The game is faster: threats were tuned against a 3.8 run and may want retiming after playtest. The ledge grab stays (our flair, and Dead Cells' own arrival assistance). The test bot double jumps across platforming flights. Their numbers are ours, marked as tuning, never claimed as Motion Twin's.
