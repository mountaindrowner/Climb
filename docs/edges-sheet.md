# The edges of every level: one sheet

*Your word, 27 Nov: "Let's continue the expansions of every level at the edges of the wings. And if possible, let's
push every level up a bit, creating space for more at the base or above to expand. Do all the levels and expand
well." (Roadmap, decision 0.36.)*

*You said to do them all, so I am building as I go, level by level, and not waiting on this sheet. It is here so
that you can stop or change any one of them before I reach it. What is built is marked "as built", with what came
out different.*

## What "the edges" means here

- **Past each wing's far end** there is a further place: a few screens, of that wing, that go where the wing was
  always pointing.
- **Under each level and over it** there is now room (build 115: two rooms of rock under, one room of space over).
  At least one far place in each level goes down into it or up into it.
- **Each far place has what decisions 0.32 and 0.33 ask of a wing:** a thing to see, a working whose lever is not
  beside what it moves, and something to find. They use the wing's own workings in a new arrangement before they
  add anything new.
- **None of them is on the way up.** The climb stays the length it is.

## Two new things in the engine, used in several levels

1. **A thing that moves only once its winch is turned** (build 116). Until now everything that moves and can be
   stood on went by the world's clock from the first. Now a bucket-chain, a lift or a planet can stand still until
   he finds what starts it, and his save keeps it going.
2. **A save that follows its level** (build 115). A level can grow west or down without a line being written for
   old saves each time.

### 1. The Roots

**West: the Drawing-Place.** *(`docs/world.md` has had this room on its list since the first plan: "where the
Well's keepers drew water; buckets on chains.")* The keepers did one thing: they drew water here and carried it up.

- **A well-shaft three screens tall,** west of the weir, with the keepers' bucket-chain hanging in it. The chain is
  still. The shaft is sheer: there is no climbing it.
- **Its windlass is at the foot,** in the sump under the shaft, reached from the floor of the cells' pit.
- **Wound, the buckets go up one side.** He rides them to **the loft** at the top: where the water was tipped into
  the carriers' yokes. A thread, a chest, the keepers' words on the wall, and a drop back to the weir's east bank.
- **Down: the Sounding.** In the sump's floor is the mouth of the Well itself: water, straight down, three screens
  into the new rock. The keepers sounded it with a weighted line and it is still hanging there. For one who has the
  Seated's Wind and can go under. At the bottom: the lead, a thread, and what they wrote when the line ran out.

**East: the Unsorted.** The house had more dead than room. Past the wall of niches is where they were put when the
sorting fell behind: a pit, tipped full, going down under the Ossuary.

- **One scale, three stacks.** The pan is at the bottom of the pit. Its slab shuts the way out at the pit's foot.
- **Each stack is one piece,** on a ledge of its own, at three heights round the pit. The slab rises a third for
  each. So he has to find all three ledges, and they are not on one path.
- **Two Tallymen keep the pan.** What he knocks in, they carry back.
- **Behind the slab:** the foot of the pit runs back west under the aisle to the well's foot: a short way out, and a
  chest on it. A thread on the highest ledge.

### 2. The Cloister

**West: the Empty Shelves.** Past the copying hall is where the finished books were to go. No book was ever
finished. Three screens of shelves, floor to roof, with nothing on them.

- **The rolling ladders,** from the sheet you approved (`docs/bare-wings-sheet.md`, section 2), built here, where
  there is a place made for them, and not squeezed into the hall. Struck, a ladder runs along its rail to its other
  stop. Where it stops it is the way up.
- **Two ladders, and the rail of each goes out of sight** through the shelving. The second cannot be reached until
  the first has been sent.
- **Up: the binders' loft,** over the rubrication, in the room above the level. Presses, thread, covers with nothing
  in them. A thread, a chest.

**East: the Cull.** Past the molds: where the bells that came out wrong were thrown, down a chute into a pit below
the slag runs.

- **Dumb bells.** A cracked bell struck here does not ring. Tongues still hang among them, and those do.
- **A casting crane on a chain** carries him over the pit, once its winch is turned. The winch is at the bottom,
  among the slag.
- **Down: the cooling pit,** in the new rock. A thread, a chest.

### 3. The Spires

**West: the Way Down.** Everyone who built a cairn then went down. Past his cairn is the path they took: it goes
down the mountain's west shoulder, four screens, and stops at a place where they turned to look back.

- **It is one way:** each drop is too tall to climb again.
- **A wind-break at the bottom.** Down, its wind runs back up the whole path. It is the way back, and it is at the
  far end.
- **At the look-back:** the climb he came up, seen across the gulf. A thread, a chest.

**East: the Crevasse.** Past the Seated the glacier is split. The crack goes down three screens, blue to black.

- **One fire, far from its ice.** A brazier on a ledge near the top; what it melts is a plug at the bottom. And a
  second, at the bottom, that opens the ledge near the top where the thread is.
- **Climbers in the walls.** One of them is in the ice the first fire melts.
- **A thread in the ice, a chest at the bottom.**

  the wheels and opens it again.

  through the houses, past the things that are easy to knock over.
