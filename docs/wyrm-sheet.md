## The proposal

### 1. A stage of its own: the broken pass

Not a floor. The summit is a ridge the Wyrm has broken: three levels to stand on, and open air between. He has the
air dash by now, and the fight is built so that he needs it.

```
                    ___________                      high: one narrow slab in the middle
        ______                       ______          middle: a shelf either side
  ___________________     ___________________        the pass: two halves, a gap between them
  ///////////////////  ~  ///////////////////        the gap: a drop into cloud (a fall, one heart, back on the pass)
```

- **The gap in the middle** is three metres: a jump or a dash. The Wyrm comes up through it.
- **The wind of the Spires blows here too,** between its attacks, from half health: a gust along the pass that
  carries him toward the gap. The region's own rule, in its keeper's hall.

### 2. Its attacks: ice from its mouth, and its flight

Each attack takes one level away from him. He reads which, and goes to another.

| Attack | The warning | What it does | The answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Frost breath** (new) | It hangs at one end, draws breath: its jaw opens, snow streams into it (0.8 s) | A blast of frost along **the pass**. That half of the floor is ice spikes for three seconds | Be on a shelf or the slab. Its head is low and still while it breathes: **strike it from the shelf above** |
| **The pass-through** (new) | It leaves: small, far off, turning (as in its coming). A line of driven snow shows which level (1 s) | It comes out of the distance and through the stage along **one level** (the pass, the shelves, or the slab), the whole width | Be on another level. Or dash across its line |
| **Ice from its mouth** (new) | Its head rears, three motes of light in its jaw (0.5 s) | Three shards of ice, one after another, aimed at where he stands. Each **cracks, with a sound**, where it lands and leaves a spike a second | Keep moving. A shard can be struck back at it with a parry |
| **Plunge** (kept) | A red mark follows him | Its head is driven into whatever he stands on, and is stuck: **the opening** | Get out from under, then hit it |
| **Up through the gap** (from half health) | The cloud in the gap boils | It comes straight up through the gap and arcs over to one side | Stay off the gap's edges |

- **From half health:** the wind; two attacks run together (a breath, then at once a pass-through); the plunge's
  spikes as today.
- **The sweep along the floor is gone.** The pass-through replaces it, and uses its flight.
- **Its openings:** stuck after a plunge (as today), and from above while it breathes. Two, and each has to be
  earned by being in the right place.
- **Health stays 38.** With two openings instead of a head lying in front of him after most attacks, the fight is
  about twice as long as today.

### 3. Its coming: from the far peak

It already comes out of the distance, but for a second and a half, and only once he is on the summit. Two changes:

- **It is seen before the fight.** From the eyrie and the span, the last two rooms of the climb, it is on the far
  peak in the sky behind: small, coiled, and it lifts its head when he comes into view.
- **At the summit it leaves that peak.** The far part of its coming is three seconds, not one and a half: it
  uncoils from the peak, crosses the sky toward him growing, and then the pass, the circle and the roar as today.

### 4. Sound

Every piece of ice gets a sound: the breath (a long hiss of wind and grit), a shard leaving its jaw, a shard
landing (a crack), spikes rising, and its wings on a pass-through (a rush that comes from one side and leaves by the
other).

### 5. Its look

It is a puppet of flat pieces: a head with a jaw, sixteen rings, a tail. I would keep the puppet (it is what lets
it fly and coil), and have **new pieces drawn**: a head with horns and a frill, larger; rings with a ridge of ice
along the back; a tail with a blade. **I will show you the pieces before they go in,** as with any creature.

### 6. What it leaves

The glide is to move to the Seated, and the Wyrm is to leave its weapon, the tooth (a spear of its ice). Neither is
part of this build. Until then it leaves what it leaves today.
