# The side view (branch `claude/climb-2d`)

The Climb as a 2.5D side-scroller: a flat plane that climbs up through shafts, tunnels and chambers, metroidvania-style, with HD pixel art from PixelLab and a bright, glowing look. Open `side.html`.

- **World** (`src/side/level.js`): tiles half a unit square (32 pixels), rooms a screen (40 x 23 tiles) or several, joined into a region by doorways, shafts and tunnels. Rooms are built from pieces (walls, doors, ledges, stairs, low tunnels, spikes) and markers (spawn, torches, crystals, creatures, the shrine, the boss, the exit). The first region is twelve rooms on a 4 x 6 grid: up from the start through the corridor, shaft and tunnel to the chamber; then either right along the ascent and gallery to the foot of the well, or up through the roost and across the bridge to the grotto (the shrine) and into the well halfway up; the well climbs two screens to the Warden's arena.
- **Creatures** (`src/side/enemies.js`): crawler, flitter, spitter, burrower, the climb's threats remade for the plane, each with a tell (a red ! over it) and PixelLab strips.
- **The Warden** (`src/side/boss.js`): a stone titan at the back of a two-screen arena, only its head and chest above the stage. Its arms are three PixelLab pieces (upper arm, forearm, fist) between shoulder, elbow and fist, bent by a two-bone solve and turned to the camera, so they reach out of the depth onto the stage. It slams (the fist follows him, holds over a red mark, drops), sweeps the stage (double-jump it, roll through it, or stand on the high ledges), and from half health its slams ripple along the stage and stones fall from the roof. Its fists can be cut while they rest on the stage; a plunge onto one hurts more. The camera draws back for the fight. It wakes when he stands on the stage and starts over if he dies; when it falls, the way up opens.
- **Shrine**: fills the flask and the hearts, and is where he wakes after a death.
- **Motor** (`src/side/motor.js`): Movement v2 on tile collision. Same tuning (`TUNE.move`), the same F9 feel panel and input queue as the climb: the double jump, launch float, air-control fade, coyote time, jump buffer, roll cancels, plunge, heavy landings, the ledge grab; and walls, ceilings, one-way ledges (down + jump drops through), steps climbed as you walk, low tunnels (crouch or roll), spikes.
- **Look** (`src/side/render.js`): rendered pixel-exact at 64 pixels a unit and scaled up whole; a narrow perspective camera so the stone has depth and the background slides past slower; PixelLab's side-scroller corner tiles on the dual grid (rock interiors darkened), the painted cavern far behind, torches and crystals with real light, and bloom on everything bright.
- **Art** (`tools/pixellab.py`): characters, side-view animations, backgrounds and tilesets from PixelLab, pulled through the API's own exports and packed into strips (`assets/side/<name>/<anim>.png` + `sprite.json` with where the feet and middle sit). The token is the session's, added by its network proxy; nothing secret is in the repository. `assets/side/pixellab.json` records what was made.

Why PixelLab over rendering our 3D models to sprites (Dead Cells' own pipeline): a bake-off of the same warrior both ways; PixelLab's was crisp hand-pixel art, the render soft and muddy at the same size.
