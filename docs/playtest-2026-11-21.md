## 3. To decide before anything is built

> **Decided 21 Nov** (roadmap, phase 0, 0.12 to 0.20): the Deep shut for now; abilities from minibosses and weapons
> from keepers; a hammer, and more weapons; fall damage stays with the second jump resetting it; no map sold or
> given; three to five deaths a region; people hint and do not explain; each region wider (far left, far right, the
> main way). Still open: 4 (up to look and strike).

1. **The Deep.** You got into the Workings, the Vein and the Great Shaft early and felt you should not be there, both
   playtests. Shut it until a set point (which?), or leave it open and make it plainly too hard?
2. **What gives what.** "Abilities from minibosses, weapons from bosses"? Today the keepers give both. This changes
   the whole order of gifts, so it comes before the hammer.
3. **The hammer.** Yes to replacing the spade outright? Then it needs drawing, and a line in the lore.
4. **Up.** Should up on the keyboard stop being jump, so that up can look and strike upward? You said both.
5. **Fall damage.** Keep it, with the second jump resetting it? Or less of it?
6. **Maps.** If none are sold, does Corwen still give one as a reward, or is the map only what you have walked?
7. **How hard.** Fewer springs, less money, tougher keepers all pull the same way. How far: a few deaths a region?
8. **Hob in the Vein.** The transcript has you say you understand him, then "make it more ambiguous." Which?
   **Answered 26 Nov (roadmap 0.27):** ambiguous means not a giveaway. He does not say what the next part needs; he
   says it is tricky, that something is going on, and what to watch. And not in poems.
