**Mobile Friendly, plainly.** The game resizes to the window, shows a touch pad on a touch screen, and asks for the
phone to be turned when it is upright. I checked that in a phone-sized touch browser here (844 x 390: the pad shows, a
drag on the left of the screen moves him). I have not held it on a real phone, and the roadmap still has a phone pass
to come (phase 20). Tick it once you have played a few minutes on your own phone.

**AI disclosure, plainly.** The guide says images taken from the running game need not be marked. That does not hold
here, because the game's own art is generated: the sprites and backdrops with PixelLab, the music and sound effects
with PixelLab, the words and the code with Claude. So all four boxes. (Drawn by code, not by an image model: the title
screen's mountain, the pixel lettering, the people's mumbled voices.) Please confirm this matches what you know.
