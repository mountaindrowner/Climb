You kept the flame at the top of the mountain. You let it go out, and you fell. The water at the bottom brought you back. Far above, a light is calling you. Climb back to the top.

The Climb is a pixel-art Metroidvania played in the browser: one mountain, climbed from its roots to its summit, with a sword, a flask and whatever its keepers give up.

[insert image: desc-keeper.png]

Features

  • One mountain, eleven regions. Caves, a monastery, a frozen pass, a storm, gardens above the cloud, the summit among the stars, and the flooded mines underneath. About 180 rooms.
  • Keepers. Each region is held by one. Beat it and it gives you something: a heavy spade that breaks cracked stone, an air dash, a glide, a grip for rough rock.
  • Sword combat. Combos, a block and a parry, a roll, a rising attack and a slam. Embers from what you kill pay for stronger moves.
  • Old ground opens up. New abilities reach rooms you walked past. A map fills in as you go, and later you learn to travel between the pools you have found.
  • People on the way. A map-maker, a lamplighter who sells, and another climber. Relics to find and equip.
  • It saves itself in your browser, at checkpoint pools.

Controller: plug one in and the game shows its buttons. Phone or tablet: hold it sideways; drag on the left of the screen to move, and use the buttons on the right.

How to play

  • Go up. The way on is always above you; the mines under the mountain are optional.
  • Stand in a checkpoint pool to heal and refill your flask. If you die, you come back at the last one.
  • Pause to see the map, your inventory and your current goal.
  • Options has three difficulties, text speed, and switches for screen shake and flashes.
